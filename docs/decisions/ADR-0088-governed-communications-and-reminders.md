# ADR-0088. Governed email, personal notifications and reminders

**Proposed, 2026-11-20.** The owner chartered COMMS-1 and requires a person's approval for every outward send. Fable reads this design before implementation.

An office worker can publish “two days before the agreed booking end, notify the owner and dispatch, and prepare the customer's return email.” The same declarations can remind a case owner about a review and prepare a quote or invoice from its record. The rentals inventory (§D, outgoing email and notification center) supplies those examples. The shared capability supplies the semantics once. SMS, staff chat and Outlook can later bind their own channel, consent and delivery contracts to the same thread, participant and business-object identities. ## One message from preparation through delivery

| Lane | Owns and publishes | Consumer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CONNECT-1 | Implements the Communication capability's **one canonical outbound message**, its revisions, pending-send reader and writers, including the proof-consuming approval transaction. It also owns the separate connection decision, scoped installation, outbox intent, provider worker and verified callback mapping. | The document action, Customize, workflows and reminders all call the same prepare operation. The identity origin reads this message by ID. |
| COMMS-1 | Defines the message terms and transitions below. Implements template and reminder revisions, recipient and consent policy, thread and inbox queries, and the reusable notification and preparation bindings. | Supplies governed inputs to CONNECT-1's writer and projects its receipts into the record and thread. |
| SIGNIN-1 slice 2b | Issues the outward-send decision proof on the identity origin and joins proof consumption to CONNECT-1's writer. | A workflow approval, template approval or session login alone confers no send authority. |
| DOCS-1 | Owns document templates, clean publication, version handles, byte digests and current read/disclosure checks. | Communication owns the email subject and body template. An exact published PDF may be attached independently of how it was generated. |
| WORKFLOW-1 | Owns release-pinned runs, authority ceilings, journals, durable timers and recovery. | COMMS-1 binds its declared reminder and prepare/notify operations to that scheduler. N2-05 exercises the complete case on these contracts. |

A connector intent refers to the approved revision. The intent carries the delivery key and evidence references. The message remains the business record. CONNECT-1 can land the writer and decision reader before 2b with dispatch closed. SIGNIN-1 tests the production reader/writer join on that record before outward sends open. This splits implementation ownership without introducing a second pending-send table or a workflow-specific copy. ### What is reviewed

1. The released email action declares its source record type and governed query, ordered recipient selectors and roles, approved email template ID, purpose, document selectors and channel. A reminder or workflow selects that same action by stable ID. Its invocation names the action and saved record plus an expected revision or occurrence. Derive tenant, environment, acting company, record identity/revision, owner, customer and contact relationships from authenticated context and stored facts. Check a prepare grant and record scope. For a shared master require an authorized acting company. Resolve To/Cc only through declared, typed contact endpoints on that record or a governed relation. Refuse missing, ambiguous, hidden, suppressed, foreign-company or disallowed endpoints. The owner's COMMS ruling narrows the unshipped email action in ADR-0080 to stored sources and an approved template. Confirmation cannot add a typed address, substitute a subject/body or select an arbitrary file.
2. Store a stable thread linked to the business record and scoped participants. Each outbound occurrence has a stable message ID. Each immutable revision captures its source and query dependencies, purpose/channel, template ID/revision, exact rendered subject/body and digest, ordered recipient roles/contact IDs/endpoints, verified company sender and provider installation/account. For attachments store the ordered DOCS-1 version handles, digests and governed links to the record. Source links, scope and access are checked for each recipient's disclosure. Retain the issue and approval actors, release ID/root and activation fence. A new revision supersedes the previous review. Retry of the same preparation returns the existing revision under current authority.
3. The identity service loads ordered, labeled terms from CONNECT-1's narrow database reader. Show the rendered text, company, account/sender, purpose, every address and role, and each named attachment and exact version. Bind those terms, message/record/revision, versioned digest, attachment digests, release and fence, plus any run/occurrence, to the SIGNIN-1 outward-send challenge. Require a fresh user-verified passkey assertion for that action and outcome within five minutes. Only the isolated identity service issues the one-use proof bound to the session, person and scope.
4. Under lock the writer recomputes the same projection from stored facts, checks the proof, session and current confirm grant, and revalidates the source, consent, template, installation and exact document versions. In one transaction consume the proof, append the approval and audit, and create the trust-outbox row and one immutable send intent. Exact committed retries recover the receipt under current authority. A changed material term, grant or fence refuses. Switching release A → B → A still requires a new review. A workflow or reminder may prepare the message. A future unattended policy for a named class requires a separate owner ruling.

| Message status | Fact the user can inspect |
| --- | --- |
| drafted | A retained message revision exists. Its author can prepare a successor from governed sources. |
| awaiting approval | The exact revision is ready for a person's decision. A decline is audited and returns the message to drafted. |
| approved | The proof was consumed and its intent committed. Queued, retrying or held for reconciliation appears beside this status. |
| sent | The provider accepted the exact envelope. The receipt records its ID and acceptance time. Delivery is a distinct per-recipient fact. |
| failed | The send was definitively refused, or retries exhausted with proven non-acceptance. An uncertain acceptance stays held for reconciliation. |
| bounced | An authenticated bounce is correlated to an accepted recipient. Show the affected recipients and the outcomes of the others. Late acceptance or delivery cannot erase that bounce. |

Reconcile an in-flight or uncertain attempt before permitting a replacement occurrence. An intentional resend of a settled message creates a fresh occurrence and requires a fresh yes. Keep the original message, revisions, decision, per-recipient receipts, attempts and status history. Access to history and attachments follows current record, field and document permissions. Audit the exact send and any later outcome with scoped IDs/digests and timestamps. Keep message contents and addresses in governed storage.

## Delivery and customer preferences

Only the ADR-0080 worker sends. It derives an opaque provider key from the scoped installation, operation and occurrence, binds the exact envelope digest, and persists a fenced claim and attempt before I/O. Retries keep that key, envelope and account. At the last authorization check before handoff, recheck the release fence, approver's live rights, automation grants if applicable, verified sender, installation, consent, source-field disclosure and exact document access. Obtain the clean bytes from DOCS-1 under that scope. A stale approval or changed endpoint holds the whole envelope for a new decision. Never silently trim the reviewed recipients or substitute a newer attachment. [ADR-0078, record links and versions](ADR-0078-governed-documents-files-templates-and-signatures.md#record-links-versions-and-retention)

Use the connector's declared retryable outcomes, attempt and age caps, backoff, deadlines, quotas and provider deduplication window. After a crash following acceptance, reconcile using verified account, occurrence metadata and provider receipt. Retry only with proven non-acceptance or the original key inside its safe window. Otherwise retain the ambiguous attempt for an operator. Operator replay and restore use the same checks. An authenticated callback enters CONNECT-1's durable inbox once per scoped provider event ID. Check signature, freshness, account, stored message and recipient before mapping it. Quarantine a changed payload under the same ID or unknown correlation. Apply out-of-order events only when corroborated. [ADR-0080, delivery and inbound correlation](ADR-0080-connector-framework.md#from-a-committed-change-to-a-provider-receipt)

Each customer-facing recipient requires current permission for that company, channel, endpoint and declared purpose. Keep append-only consent and suppression events with provenance and effective time. Customer mail offers a provider-supported unsubscribe bound to its recipient and purpose. Consume its authenticated event through the same inbox, resolve its scope from stored correspondence, and record suppression idempotently. A hard bounce suppresses the endpoint across email purposes for that company, including another contact alias for the same address. A temporary bounce records its classification and follows the provider contract. A later delivery receipt cannot opt a recipient back in. Restore eligibility only through an authorized, evidenced consent or endpoint correction appropriate to the suppression. Serialize suppression with dispatch admission. A withdrawal committed before admission prevents the send, and one received after handoff governs future sends. Preserve the original sent record in both cases.

Real provider calls, including connection and health checks, require the explicitly enabled local demo until SIGNIN-1 proves its decision gates and CONNECT-1 admits pilot delivery. Every environment still requires a person's yes to send. CI uses network-free fakes with an acceptance ledger. Bind credentials only through ADR-0080's scoped installation slots resolved by the host at use time. The local demo uses its owner-readable file via `NORTH_STAR_CONNECTOR_KEY_FILE`. Patches, messages, logs, browser and assistant receive no credential values. [ADR-0080, company connections](ADR-0080-connector-framework.md#who-can-connect-a-company)

## What an owner can publish in Customize

Two versioned, closed patch members append a template revision or a reminder-rule revision. Each includes its stable logical ID, new revision identity, expected predecessor (absent on creation), scope and an active or retired disposition. “Edit” appends content to the same ID. “Retire” appends a tombstone. Reject stale predecessors, reused revision IDs with different bytes and live references to a retired member. Permit coordinated replacement or retirement in an ordered change set. The active release selects one revision per ID. History retains the originally used revisions. The normal draft, plain diff, verify, approve and activate path applies to both human and assistant authors.

An email template binds its source query and record type, permitted purpose, bounded literal text and typed field placeholders in subject and plain-text body. The compiler checks that the action's source and purpose match the template and that each selected destination may receive the rendered content and visible recipient list. Bind fields by stable ID. Permit only approved query output and classifications for each output position. Reject undeclared paths, raw markup, expressions, header controls and unbounded values. Recheck every referenced field on one governed read snapshot for **the issuing person** at preview and issue. Refuse the render if any merged field is hidden or its source truncated or inaccessible. Preview produces neither a message nor an intent. For workflow preparation use the bounded automation principal and its live grant ceiling. Check the human reviewer's current read and disclosure rights before showing any text, and recheck the captured dependencies before dispatch. Template activation confers no access to the data it renders. [ADR-0078, governed template bindings](ADR-0078-governed-documents-files-templates-and-signatures.md#templates-and-pdf)

A reminder rule binds a stable record/occurrence query and date or instant anchor, eligibility predicate, positive bounded lead and unit, calendar source, recipient selectors (record owner or named company roles), and effects (inbox and/or prepare through a released email action). Record the actual role and owner resolutions at issue. A tenant can bind a governed event to the same internal notify operation in a release-authored workflow, selecting the record owner or a named role. The host checks the pinned operation grant and journals the occurrence. An inbox action can link to the saved record or its human task. The inbox is a scoped List per signed-in person. Deduplicate the owner who also holds the selected role. Check each person's active company membership and source-record/field grants before creating an entry, and recheck them for rows, counts, previews and links on every read. Only its recipient can mark an entry read or archive it. The internal path needs no outward approval and creates no provider intent.

## When a reminder fires

Use the workflow scheduler's durable timer, journal and verified active fence. For “N days before,” subtract N **civil dates** in the source's declared business calendar, retaining the local time or using the declared business-day boundary for a date-only anchor. Resolve each date independently in the pinned IANA zone. An elapsed-hour lead subtracts hours from the anchor instant. A working-day rule requires a governed, versioned list of working dates including exceptions for the whole horizon. Refuse that unit when this data is unavailable. Never assume Monday–Friday. Capture the anchor identity, source revision, calendar version/zone, boundary, lead and resolved UTC deadline. Require an explicit calendar resolution for an ambiguous civil boundary and surface an unresolvable gap or missing calendar for correction. [Tenant business day](ADR-0018-temporal-authority.md#the-tenant-owns-the-business-day) · [Workflow timers](ADR-0073-workflow-isolate-and-authority.md#timers-signals-and-external-delivery)

Identify one natural occurrence by tenant, environment, company, stable rule ID, record, and the source's stable business occurrence/anchor slot (e.g. this booking's agreed end). Keep revisions, timer IDs, deadlines, recipient membership and release fences out of this uniqueness key. A genuinely recurring source supplies a distinct stored cycle ID. Editing the date, lead or calendar reschedules a pending occurrence. Retirement, cancellation or loss of eligibility closes pending work. A fence change suspends old work. Re-evaluate under the newly verified release before arming a successor against the same occurrence key. An already emitted occurrence remains consumed.

At wake, lock the occurrence, compare the expected timer generation, re-read current eligibility and membership, and resolve recipients once. Atomically commit the occurrence, one inbox row per distinct eligible person and effect slot, and any pending email via CONNECT-1's prepare writer. Journal the result so replay returns its IDs. No network I/O occurs at wake. An unsatisfied declared email binding yields an inspectable refusal and no partial fan-out. Recover overdue work once while still eligible and before the anchor, otherwise record a missed occurrence. Bound catch-up batches, retain a durable cursor and rescan after restart. Use database instants for due work and an injected monotonic clock for elapsed worker budgets.

## Admission and evidence at implementation

- Give new tables scoped keys, foreign keys and RLS for tenant, environment and company. Grant runtime access through scoped functions only. Writers use narrow SECURITY DEFINER functions with `search_path = pg_catalog, pg_temp`, qualified names, explicit execute grants and stored-target write authority. Check READ COMMITTED on every writer that depends on it, including retire, archive and sweep. Follow one lock order across the participating writers and workers, including consent and revocation. Bound sweep claims with SKIP LOCKED or NOWAIT and isolate each occurrence's commit. Use disjoint system and user keyspaces.
- Emit optional declarations and their projections only when used. Verify both directions between declarations, projections, operations, queries, permissions and required surface floor. For **each** new incompatible capability allocate the next scalar rung on freshly fetched main at its executable merge, regenerate, run CI and re-fetch before push. Allocate any migration the same way. A release takes the maximum it needs. Exercise the archived preceding production loader before serving or claiming, and the prior parser on standalone and nested members before applying. Compare persisted first-party lineage and a real tenant patch chain without these features across both builds, including complete release bytes, roots and floors.
- Apply ADR-0085 to each patch kind at introduction. Add it to the independent replay allowlist and named test inventory. Reproduce every original revision/root, then replay unchanged ordered patches on a compatible base. Exercise each kind's real conflict, including a retired field, role or incompatible scope. Name the patch, member and changed binding and preserve verified serving. Pointer rollback retains history and invalidates outstanding approvals. Deployment must support the highest parser and reader used by any tenant, including after rollback or restore. [ADR-0085, replay and deployment](ADR-0085-platform-upgrades-for-customized-tenants.md#slice-1-replay-admission)
- In future executable slices use the actual runtime roles, two companies in one tenant/environment, and the correct record owner so that company scope alone decides. Exercise the approval and egress gates independently, remove one guard at a time after committing the control, and observe its own failure. Prove idempotency by counting actual fake-provider acceptances and persisted inbox/occurrence rows. Prove reuse with a customized booking and an unrelated approval case through the published operations, including a provider failure and recovery.
