# ADR-XXXX. Live location and estimated arrival for one trip

**Proposed · TRACK-1 slice 0 · 2026-11-21.** Assign the number at merge. This is the design for independent read. The owner has ruled that each company chooses whether a message rule sends a link automatically or asks a person.

| Business | What someone can do with the same blocks |
| --- | --- |
| Delivery | A driver taps **Share during my shift**. Dispatch sees the van approach stop A. The company's rule sends B a link when B becomes next. If dispatch issued B an invitation earlier, B sees an unavailable page throughout A's delivery. |
| Rentals | A yard clerk declares **Compressor** trackable and pairs unit 17 with its GPS device. The yard can locate the unit during the rental. For its return, the receiving depot sees the position and estimated arrival for that hand-off. The link expires when the hand-off closes. |
| Field service | A technician shares from a phone for a boiler visit. The office chooses **Review before sending**. When the technician locks the phone, fresh fixes stop and the customer sees the age of the last update, followed by **Live position unavailable**. Ending the shift stops both collection and live disclosure. |

The owner's rentals survey (inventory §§A–B, “Driver and customer tracking” and “Fleet, jobsite, and dispatch maps”) identifies driver position on fleet maps and a customer-facing `tracking.html`. That page's estimated-arrival and last-update text translate to all three businesses. Its “Stops before you” counter would disclose other work. The governing plan puts surfaces in the compiled runtime, dispatch invariants in Tier B, and device adapters and bounded automation behind governed contracts. ## 1. Declare what can move

Give any first-party or company-authored record type a trackable declaration by stable type ID. The existing record is the subject. Keep telemetry in a scoped companion so enabling this on a protected type never grants edits to its business facts. The compiler requires a supported exact-read and ownership contract. A shared master has a separate tracking identity in each expressly authorized acting company. A protected type lacking an admissible binding gets a diagnostic naming the missing contract. A driver table would force a rental company to invent a driver for a compressor.

| --- | --- |

| `addTripTrackingRevision` | Trip/visit type, to-one subject and destination relations, current assignment and destination point queries, period, lifecycle operations and workflow event contract. An optional Planner layer binds this scoped query. |

Record the choices once in add-only, predecessor-linked revisions. Labels resolve from the serving release. Refuse broken, ambiguous or retired active references with the offending ID. The visual builder and the in-app AI emit the same ordered patches. The drafter vocabulary comes from the member schema. Compare all patch kinds and variants against drafter coverage in CI. Live AI-BUILD-1 cases build the compressor return and the technician visit from plain English and exercise an incompatible binding. Both paths show the human-readable diff, verification, approval, activation and rollback.

Preserve feature-absent persisted bytes, roots and floors on first-party and patched releases. ## 2. Accept observations from an authorized source

One source-bound position operation takes a source event ID, WGS84 point, horizontal accuracy in metres (explicitly unknown if the device cannot supply it) and observed instant. Store server receipt time, scoped subject, verified source identity and kind, consent/assignment interval, and provenance linking the authenticated browser session or verified installation event to the operation receipt. This is evidence of what that source reported. The company, subject association, source class and provenance come from server-held bindings. Editable latitude/longitude fields would let a form, import or AI manufacture movement.

| Source | Admission and what the operator sees |
| --- | --- |
| Signed-in phone | A named staff member starts a finite capture session from a Task. Show who can see it, the trip/window, provider disclosure, expiry, last accepted time and **Stop sharing**. Require affirmative consent, verified principal-to-subject assignment, current duty interval and browser permission. Propose a narrow ADR-0036 §2a behaviour in the owned script to watch geolocation on a visible HTTPS page and submit bounded observations same-origin with session and CSRF checks. Clear the watch on hide, denial, stop or exit. Drop any queued callback for a closed session. A closed or locked page supplies no dependable updates. Resume visibly under a fresh eligibility check. No offline queue. With script off, status, withdrawal and manual page refresh work and the page explains that phone capture needs JavaScript. [W3C Geolocation](https://www.w3.org/TR/geolocation/#request-a-position) |
| Equipment GPS | An enabled ADR-0080 installation verifies the signed, fresh provider event, account, device association and mapping version. A narrow service grant admits the installed mapping to the same position operation. Deduplicate through the durable inbound inbox and quarantine a changed body under the same ID. Derive the unit from its effective pairing. A swapped device or delayed event cannot move the former unit. [ADR-0080, inbound delivery](ADR-0080-connector-framework.md#from-a-committed-change-to-a-provider-receipt) |
| Installed app, later | The same observation contract admits a separately authenticated and revocable installation after a background-permission and visible-stop design on the target OS. Declaring this source before an adapter exists refuses at compile time. Installing the web page alone offers no locked-screen promise. |

Serialize observation admission with assignment, consent and duty changes. Check eligibility at observation time **and** commit time. For any source that identifies a person, including a tracker tied to an assigned worker, apply that person's consent, audience, and active period. At withdrawal, sign-out of phone capture or shift end, close that capture and suppress its point and derived ETA on live reads. A new active period needs fresh consent to capture and its own eligible observation.

A device-only equipment purpose may use a declared rental or custody interval. Relabelling a person or binding their phone to an asset cannot remove the human checks. Require authoritative identity and period bindings or refuse collection.

Use one selected active source per subject and purpose. Changing it advances the source binding. Validate finite coordinates, accuracy, event age and future skew. Order eligible observations by observed time with a committed sequence as tie-break. A late observation cannot move a marker back.

Exact retries under current authority return one receipt. Changed input under a used key refuses. Apply bounded body and request limits at entry, then atomic limits per source, subject and company before durable position writes or paid work. Proposed starting limits are one new fix per subject per 15 seconds and 600 per company per minute, with `429` and retry advice. Passive fixes never extend a login. The operation requires source-bound authority even on direct gateway calls. Generic edits, workflows, imports and assistant tools cannot obtain it. The AI can query currently authorized positions under row, field and purpose grants. Its read must also pass the same live-period and retention checks. Revoked or wrong-company reads match not-found.

## 3. A visit has one subject, one destination and an accountable state

Use the declared trip instance and stable ID for one leg. Derive scope, assignment, destination and revisions from stored records. **Plan → Start → Arrive → Finish** are Semantic Operations for **Planned → On the way → Arrived → Done**. The caller needs the transition's write grant. Compare expected revision and release fence, then commit the transition, receipt, audit and one versioned outbox event together. A workflow may invoke the same transition under its own grants. An identical retry yields one occurrence. A state-change event names the scoped trip, prior and new state, event ID and committed revision. It carries no coordinates, recipient address or invitation. GPS proximity alone would mistake passing a jobsite for arrival.

For a delivery, bind to ROUTES-1's **published** manifest, exact stop and confirmed progress. Draft plans and the ordering of visible pins confer no access. Dispatch remains the sole writer of its progress. Its transition event drives the tracking view idempotently. For a standalone service visit or rental return, the declared visit operations own the state without needing a dispatch route. Reassignment, destination/contact change, manifest replacement or cancellation closes the old sharing authority. A replacement trip or binding requires a fresh grant. Updated estimates never reorder visits or change appointments. [ROUTES-1 §§5–6 at ba1e1493](https://github.com/AnserBeg/2rain-greenfield/blob/ba1e14937117a4767bcc6644c9b9d97931cef2b9/docs/decisions/ADR-XXXX-route-optimisation.md#6-arrival-estimates-and-driver-work)

## 4. Estimate the active leg once for its viewers

An enabled company installation runs a bounded, read-only **live leg ETA** operation from the latest eligible point to this trip's current destination. Google traffic-aware routing or Valhalla is an installation choice. A company can use Valhalla for ROUTES-1 planning matrices and Google for this live leg. Display **Estimated arrival**, the time and zone, calculation time, position age, and whether traffic was used. Valhalla's ordinary road estimate carries no live-traffic claim. A straight-line guess or a scheduled appointment presented as current would give a customer a false arrival time.

Extend ADR-0080 for this specific periodic read. The authorized company decision covers coordinates, service, purpose, region, permitted display/cache and a finite spend and rate ceiling. Resolve credentials only on the host. Recheck current consent, classification, source rights, trip/fence, verified installation, entitlement and atomic cost reservation before egress. Restrict the request to that leg and approved road profile. A missing entitlement, exhausted budget or unreachable leg gives **Estimate unavailable**. Never silently switch providers. CI uses network-free adapters. Public page loads, reconnects and AI reads make zero routing calls.

Coalesce fresh fixes per trip, at most one routing attempt per minute and one in flight. Key the scoped cache by trip/binding revision, source observation, destination revision, installation, routing profile and departure-time bucket. At write-back compare the captured binding, destination, fence and observation sequence under lock. Only advance the result for that leg and input generation. Discard late results after a change, including consent withdrawal.

Between refreshes reuse the last eligible result with its **own** input timestamp, subject to the same disclosure, retention and provider limits. Proposed maximum ages are two minutes for a live point or the point behind an ETA, and two minutes from calculation for an ETA. A fresh computation on an old point cannot reset its age. At the first limit or a passed ETA, clear the affected pin or estimate and show its unavailable text and last-update time only while disclosure remains authorized. A provider that disallows the required cache or display is ineligible.

## 5. Give one contact a window onto one trip

Extend the ADR-0081 named-contact invitation with the sole purpose **view this trip**. Bind its random grant ID to tenant, environment, company, named contact, trip, subject, destination and their assignment revisions, the published share rule, release fence, audience and finite expiry. Issue or revoke through a scoped, audited operation. Cap the invitation at 24 hours and the trip/active-period end, whichever comes first. Route replacement and a release switch away and back invalidate the old grant. The company may issue the invitation in advance. An attempt outside the visibility window returns unavailable without consuming it. Treating the URL alone as permission would let an unintended recipient follow a worker. [ADR-0081, customer and contact links](ADR-0081-staff-sign-in-and-decision-authority.md#customer-and-contact-links)

Use the identity service's fragment invitation, clean redemption page, protected exchange, separately verified contact and one-use redemption. Race two redemptions and obtain at most one host-only, short-lived, read-only session. The session cannot outlive its grant or open staff queries. Use clean application URLs, private no-store responses and a no-referrer policy. Keep invitation secrets and cookies out of logs, analytics and assistant context. Keep coordinates out of logs, traces and analytics. The assistant receives location only through a granted, purpose-bound read. The map loads only on a clean, post-redemption view.

Every initial page, fragment, stream admission, stream refresh and reconnect applies the same live grant, consent and scope tests. Known but unauthorized, unknown, wrong-company, expired, revoked or outside-window links return the same 404 and unavailable body as a missing trip on that entry path.

Customize chooses a bounded visibility predicate from released facts, for example **On the way to this visit** or **While this published stop is next and the trip is on the way**. Intersect it with the platform's active subject, current recipient association and trip limits. Unknown, ambiguous or unreadable inputs close the window. Dispatch answers whether this exact stop is next over the entire published manifest. The contact query consumes only that eligibility decision. Only a point observed within this trip's current open window may appear or contribute to the customer's ETA. At a stop change B starts empty until a new eligible fix arrives. It can never inherit A's doorstep as its last known position.

The contact query's entire projection is the trip's customer-safe label and state, permitted current point at declared precision, time/accuracy, and scoped estimated arrival. Choose the customer label from fields approved for this recipient and purpose. Default to a coarse point (§8). The server reduces precision before serialization or map display. A coarse area covers at least the reported uncertainty. For unknown accuracy show that fact and withhold the customer marker. The query has no customer-supplied subject selector, route list, path history, employee identity, other customer, stop counts or staff Record links.

Audit grant issue, redemption, view/refresh, stream access, refusal, expiry and revocation with scoped identifiers, decision, audience and time. Commit the access evidence before disclosing data. Audit failure closes the read. Unknown tokens get a bounded anonymous refusal record.

Load no third-party resource on invitation exchange. ## 7. Let the company decide how to send

The trip emits a state change even when the company has no communication rule. A COMMS-1 rule can bind **On the way → prepare this trip's link for its stored contact**. The company chooses **Await approval** or **Send automatically** per rule. A hard-wired text on Start would bypass the service firm's dispatcher and surprise a yard that tracks equipment only for its own staff.

Apply the November 21 COMMS-1 owner ruling to ADR-0088's earlier per-send requirement. COMMS-1 owns that amendment and mode. TRACK-1 supplies the event and one-trip grant. A human may issue the invitation, or the approved automatic rule may request issuance under a bounded company service grant for this exact purpose through the identity issuer. This proposes a narrow extension to ADR-0081 staff issuance. Only an authorized company administrator can enable automatic mode through normal reviewed Customize publication. Pin trigger, rule revision, template, recipient relation, purpose, channel, sending installation, permitted scope, quotas, expiry and activation fence. The AI can draft a rule. It cannot publish that authority or mint a grant by asking to send.

Both modes use the same canonical message, exact recipient and template revision, consent, purpose, endpoint suppression, unsubscribe, audit, outbox and connector receipts.

The manual path uses SIGNIN-1's exact-envelope proof. If either mode or its grant is unavailable, refuse that rule before activation. The automatic path requires the live rule grant for that envelope and records the enabling decision and automation principal. Bind invitation and message occurrence to the committed event, rule and recipient. Retry returns the same occurrence and invitation. The COMMS template binds the issuer's link handle as a typed merge value for this recipient and trip. The envelope digest covers the exact rendered invitation and unsubscribe target. Keep exact delivery material in protected delivery storage with a redacted link handle in ordinary history and AI reads.

Recheck recipient, window, revocation, consent, suppression, installation, rule and fence at handoff. Terminally void an unsent stale intent. Reconcile uncertain provider acceptance before a retry. An unsubscribe before handoff prevents egress in either mode. Unsubscribing suppresses later mail. **Revoke trip access** closes the already-issued link and its session. ## 8. Expire the coordinates and preserve the decision record

Give each company a finite history setting. Propose **24 hours** from observation by default, including the “latest” copy. Quote the retention window when the person consents. Keep raw telemetry and derived location-bearing payloads in expiring storage separate from permanent business state. Write opaque references into receipts, trip events, audits, inbox deduplication and workflow journals. Use protected short-lived payload references where a connector inbox needs the original fix. Permanent audit of raw coordinates would defeat the company's setting.

Record the deadline at capture. A shorter policy takes effect on existing points and caches. Extension applies to future captures only. Ordinary reads refuse expired data immediately. Encrypt coordinates with expiry-scoped keys so a backup cannot recover them after key destruction. Expire history, current projections, caches and inbound payloads together. A human retention administrator previews and approves the bounded policy and its impact. A bounded worker executes it idempotently, verifies removal and records non-location tombstones. Honor a separately authorized hold in restricted storage with a finite review date and an audited release. Restore reapplies expiry, holds, revocations and the purge ledger before serving. Business trip states, access decisions and send receipts retain their own lifecycle. [ADR-0010, lifecycle and purge](ADR-0010-lifecycle-audit-correction-and-recovery.md) · [ADR-0019, restore](ADR-0019-tenant-completeness-and-single-tenant-recovery.md)

### Owner questions for the independent read

| Question | Recommended answer and reason |
| --- | --- |
| How much raw history should a new installation keep? | Start at 24 hours, configurable from 1 hour to 7 days. A dispatcher can investigate a missed visit without accumulating weeks of staff movements. |
| How precise should the first customer view be? | Default to a 250 m grid with an approximate-area marker. Allow finer disclosure only by explicit company choice and matching subject consent. Never present precision better than the source reports. |
| When should a quiet feed disappear? | Use two minutes as the maximum live age for position and ETA inputs. After that, show the last update time and **unavailable**. Test the cutoff with a technician who locks their phone. |
