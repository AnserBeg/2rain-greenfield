## 2. Accept observations from an authorized source

One source-bound position operation takes a source event ID, WGS84 point, horizontal accuracy in metres (explicitly unknown if the device cannot supply it) and observed instant. Store server receipt time, scoped subject, verified source identity and kind, consent/assignment interval, and provenance linking the authenticated browser session or verified installation event to the operation receipt. This is evidence of what that source reported. The company, subject association, source class and provenance come from server-held bindings. Editable latitude/longitude fields would let a form, import or AI manufacture movement.

Before deduplication and again at commit, require the same open interval/generation at observation and admission, the bound source's write grant and, for browser capture, its current lease. Reject a queued device event, late callback or exact retry from a closed interval even after re-pairing or fresh consent. Apply a person's consent, audience and active period to every source that identifies them. Withdrawal, phone sign-out or shift end closes capture and suppresses its point and derived ETA. A new active period needs fresh consent and a fresh observation.

A device-only equipment purpose may use a declared rental or custody interval. Relabelling a person or binding their phone to an asset cannot remove the human checks. Require authoritative identity and period bindings or refuse collection.

Use one selected active source per subject and purpose. Changing it advances the source binding. Require finite WGS84 coordinates in range and accuracy in (0, 10,000] metres or explicit unknown. At receipt reject observations over two minutes old or over five seconds ahead of server time. Cap the effective observation instant at receipt for ordering, freshness and retention. Across all workers, atomically reserve one new fix per source per 15 seconds, one per subject per 15 seconds and 600 per company per minute before committing a point or scheduling paid work. An exhausted bucket returns `429` with retry advice. Passive fixes never extend login. Direct gateway calls require the source-bound write action on the stored target. Generic forms, imports, workflows and assistant tools cannot obtain it. AI reads need live row, field and purpose grants and the same period and retention checks. Revoked or wrong-company reads match not-found.

## 3. A visit has one subject, one destination and an accountable state

Use the declared trip instance and stable ID for one leg. Derive scope, assignment, destination and revisions from stored records. **Plan → Start → Arrive → Finish** are Semantic Operations for **Planned → On the way → Arrived → Done**. The caller needs the transition's write grant. Compare expected revision and release fence, then commit the transition, receipt, audit and one versioned outbox event together. A workflow may invoke the same transition under its own grants. An identical retry yields one occurrence. A state-change event names the scoped trip, prior and new state, event ID and committed revision. It carries no coordinates, recipient address or invitation. GPS proximity alone would mistake passing a jobsite for arrival.

For a delivery, bind to ROUTES-1's **published** manifest, exact stop and confirmed progress. Draft plans and the ordering of visible pins confer no access. Dispatch remains the sole writer of its progress. Its transition event drives the tracking view idempotently. For a standalone service visit or rental return, the declared visit operations own the state without needing a dispatch route. Reassignment, destination/contact change, manifest replacement or cancellation closes the old sharing authority. A replacement trip or binding requires a fresh grant. Updated estimates never reorder visits or change appointments. The company selects an installation revision independently for each use. Defaults are Valhalla for planning and Google traffic-aware for live ETA. Enabling either use requires an authorized installation.

Recalculation, cache hits, renewal and reconnect preserve the original input clock. Show unavailable for the expired item. Last-update labels require continuing authorization.

## 5. Give one contact a window onto one trip

Extend the ADR-0081 named-contact invitation with the sole purpose **view this trip**. Bind its random grant ID to tenant, environment, company, named contact, trip, subject, destination and their assignment revisions, the published share rule, release fence, audience and finite expiry. Issue or revoke through a scoped, audited operation. Cap the invitation at 24 hours and the trip/active-period end, whichever comes first. Route replacement and a release switch away and back invalidate the old grant. The company may issue the invitation in advance. An attempt outside the visibility window returns unavailable without consuming it. Treating the URL alone as permission would let an unintended recipient follow a worker. [ADR-0081, customer and contact links](ADR-0081-staff-sign-in-and-decision-authority.md#customer-and-contact-links)

Use the identity service's fragment invitation, clean redemption page, protected exchange, separately verified contact and one-use redemption. Race two redemptions and obtain at most one host-only, short-lived, read-only session. The session cannot outlive its grant or open staff queries. Use clean application URLs, private no-store responses and a no-referrer policy. Keep invitation secrets and cookies out of logs, analytics and assistant context. Keep coordinates out of logs, traces and analytics. The assistant receives location only through a granted, purpose-bound read. The map loads only on a clean, post-redemption view.

Every initial page, fragment, stream admission, stream refresh and reconnect applies the same live grant, consent and scope tests. Known but unauthorized, unknown, wrong-company, expired, revoked or outside-window links return the same 404 and unavailable body as a missing trip on that entry path.

Customize selects a bounded predicate such as **On the way to this visit** or **While this published stop is next**. Intersect it with the subject's active period, current assignment, trip, recipient and consent. Dispatch resolves next over the whole published manifest. For standalone visits the declared Start supplies the leg. Serialize these transitions under the scoped moving subject. Permit one customer leg and window generation at a time across trips, even with overlapping company rules. Close A on hand-off, invalidate its display generation and fence queued work. Delay B's first disclosure until A's last outstanding display lease has expired. B may hold an invitation while its window is closed.

Becoming next arms B. To open it, require a new fix observed after confirmed progress to B whose **entire disclosed area**, including fix uncertainty, lies outside A's protected site. Use A's stored site boundary with a 250 m buffer, or a 250 m buffer around its verified destination point when there is no boundary. Apply the exclusion to every fix and its derived ETA for B. A return to A suppresses the whole projection, including previously cached estimates. Missing prior-site geometry, unknown accuracy or ambiguous prior movement keeps disclosure closed. On a verified first leg with no preceding visit, require a fresh fix after Start. Only the server evaluates the previous site. While the vehicle remains at A, B receives the ordinary unavailable response, including on fragment refresh, with no map frame, geometry, timestamp or ETA.

Project to the customer's approved precision **before** routing for that audience or serializing anything. Default to a fixed 250 m grid. Draw an approximate area about the grid-cell centre large enough to cover the cell and its reported uncertainty (round uncertainty up to a fixed size band). Finer precision requires an explicit company policy and matching subject consent where a person is involved. Unknown accuracy withholds marker and ETA. Use the same area's centre and scale for viewport, zoom limits, accessible text, frame messages and customer ETA origin. Use the admitted cell centre, a common departure bucket and B's destination for the estimate. Show a rounded arrival window. Never send a staff ETA from the raw origin, hidden points, bounding boxes or route geometry. Two raw points in the same area and accuracy band at the same time must yield identical customer content and frame inputs under the same routing result.

The contact query accepts only the scoped grant and returns a customer-safe trip label, permitted state, approximate area, allowed timestamps and that leg's estimate. Omit route lists, history, employee identity, other customers, stop counts and staff Record links.

Audit grant issue, redemption, view/refresh, stream access, refusal, expiry and revocation with scoped identifiers, decision, audience and time. Commit the access evidence before disclosing data. Audit failure closes the read. Unknown tokens get a bounded anonymous refusal record.

## 7. Let the company decide how to send

The trip emits a state change even when the company has no communication rule. A COMMS-1 rule can bind **On the way → prepare this trip's link for its stored contact**. The company chooses **Await approval** or **Send automatically** per rule. A hard-wired text on Start would bypass the service firm's dispatcher and surprise a yard that tracks equipment only for its own staff.

Automatic activation depends on COMMS-1 landing the November 21 amendment to ADR-0088 and the canonical worker implementing its service-grant path. Until then, refuse an automatic rule at publication. TRACK-1 supplies the event and one-trip grant. A human may issue the invitation, or the approved automatic rule may request issuance under a bounded company service grant for this exact purpose through the identity issuer. This proposes a narrow extension to ADR-0081 staff issuance. Only an authorized company administrator can enable automatic mode through normal reviewed Customize publication. Pin trigger, rule revision, template, recipient relation, purpose, channel, sending installation, permitted scope, quotas, expiry and activation fence. The AI can draft a rule. It cannot publish that authority or mint a grant by asking to send.

Both modes use the same canonical message, exact recipient and template revision, consent, purpose, endpoint suppression, unsubscribe, audit, outbox and connector receipts.

The manual path uses SIGNIN-1's exact-envelope proof. If either mode or its grant is unavailable, refuse that rule before activation. The automatic path requires the live rule grant for that envelope and records the enabling decision and automation principal. Persist an occurrence keyed by company, trip event, rule revision, recipient and effect, with the original activation fence and expiry. The identity issuer binds its single invitation to that occurrence and stores a recoverable, protected delivery handle. If a worker crashes after issuance and before message creation, recheck the occurrence and recover the same handle. The canonical prepare writer creates one message and outbox intent under that occurrence key. Terminal receipts and revocation tombstones prevent reissue even after delivery material expires. The COMMS template binds the issuer's link handle as a typed merge value for this recipient and trip. The envelope digest covers the exact rendered invitation and unsubscribe target. Keep exact delivery material in protected delivery storage with a redacted link handle in ordinary history and AI reads.

At the canonical CONNECT-1 worker’s last check before provider handoff, serialize consent and endpoint suppression with dispatch admission. Recheck the exact recipient, active trip/rule, revocation, installation, service grant or human proof, envelope digest and activation fence. An advance invitation can be sent only when its published send predicate permits it. Receipt of the message confers no access before the separate §5 visibility window opens. Terminally void an unsent stale intent. Reconcile uncertain provider acceptance before a retry. An unsubscribe before handoff prevents egress in either mode. Unsubscribing suppresses later mail. **Revoke trip access** closes the already-issued link and its session. ## 8. Expire the coordinates and preserve the decision record

The company sets raw retention to **24 hours by default**, configurable from **1 hour to 7 days** inclusive. Refuse publication outside that range. Quote the interval at consent. At capture, assign each point an immutable latest deadline from its effective observation time. A shorter policy immediately caps every existing deadline at observation plus the new limit, including copies awaiting purge. An extension benefits future observations only. A derived item inherits the earliest contributor deadline and any earlier provider limit. Check expiry on every read, retry, job claim and write-back, independently of the sweeper.

Keep expiring location-bearing material outside permanent business state. This includes current/history projections, estimates and routing inputs/results, inbound bodies and retry/quarantine payloads, streams, replay snapshots, indexes, exports, saved reports, AI prompts/transcripts and generated output that could reveal the point. Keep only opaque, non-reconstructing references in durable audits, event logs, receipts, inbox keys and workflow journals. A sink, including an external AI/report service, must enforce the same deadline and revocation or receive only non-location data. Evict in-memory copies, fence outstanding work and prevent replay or rebuild from refilling them. Keep location-free trip states and send decisions under their own lifecycle.

The recovery authority stores current policy/shortening epochs, effective deadlines, purge and revocation tombstones and key-destruction records **outside the tenant restore boundary**. Encrypt payloads and their copies under externally managed erasable keys with erasure granularity sufficient for each effective deadline. Tenant backups contain ciphertext and opaque key handles only. The authority denies key release at expiry, including a shortened deadline, and destroys the key independently of tenant backup/restore. A human retention administrator approves policy changes. The idempotent purge worker verifies erasure and records non-location evidence. A separately authorized hold uses segregated storage, separate keys, a finite review date, audited hold-only access and audited release. Ordinary reads, replay, AI and workers cannot unwrap held data.

Restore into isolation. Keep reads, decryption for serving, replay, rebuild and outbound workers closed until reconciliation with that current external authority finishes. Reapply shorter deadlines, destroyed keys, revocations and holds, purge obsolete copies and verify before opening. Fail closed if the authority is unavailable. Slice 1 takes a backup while the 24-hour policy is live, shortens it to one hour, then restores at age two hours. Slice 3 repeats with a cached ETA and a redeemed invitation revoked after backup. Use a trip and grant whose own expiry is still in the future. Check the history query, cache payload, key-unwrapping result and external revocation tombstone directly, as the two-minute live view would already be empty. Exercise a second, still-unredeemed revoked invitation to distinguish revocation from one-use redemption. Verify that neither restored grant authorizes a view and neither replay nor a pending worker reconstructs the point or estimate. A held copy remains accessible only through the hold process.
