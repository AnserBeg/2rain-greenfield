# ADR-0085. Ship platform updates to customized companies

**Proposed for Fable’s design read, 2026-11-20.** UPGRADE-2, design only. The owner’s ruling on the approval amendment is pending. Build follows that ruling and the landing of CUSTOMIZE-3 and WORKFLOW-1 authoring, verification and approvals. The survival and deployment gates are due before the first real customer.

## What a company is upgrading

A tenant may run several companies in one environment. They share an active release pointer. An upgrade moves that pointer once. Each authorized company sees the first-party base and customization revision actually serving its work. A platform rollout schedules those tenant/environment transitions independently.

UPGRADE-1 already replays immutable tenant patches onto a new first-party base and stages a candidate with the verified source as its parent. CRITFIX-B keeps the source serving on refusal. CRITFIX-C distinguishes a swap from a verified generation. CRITFIX-D can re-verify the same attempt and fence or explicitly revert a failed swap without parsing the broken candidate. The proposal uses those paths for every wave member. ## Proposed amendment to ADR-0006 and ADR-0081

Today an authorized person approves each exact activation attempt. A standing instruction cannot satisfy the candidate-specific sign-in proof. **Fable’s decision question is whether a tenant may authorize a separate policy decision for a clean platform upgrade whose exact candidate has passed full pre-activation verification.** Accepting that exception requires an explicit owner ruling and corresponding edits to the plan’s human-approval language, ADR-0006 and ADR-0081. Until then their existing human decision governs every activation. [Plan §§10.1, 11.3 and 12.14](../greenfield-north-star-erp-platform-plan.md) · [ADR-0006](ADR-0006-human-controlled-release-activation.md) · [ADR-0081, decision evidence](ADR-0081-staff-sign-in-and-decision-authority.md#human-decisions-and-activation-evidence)

| Decision | Who decides and what the evidence binds |
| --- | --- |
| Set upgrade policy | Default to **Manual**. Only a human authorized for release policy across the affected tenant/environment can enable **Automatically approve clean platform upgrades**. On the identity origin show the scope, affected companies, eligible changes, exclusions and how to revoke. Add a distinct ADR-0081 decision kind. Bind its one-use, user-verified assertion to the human, scope, before/after mode, terms digest and expected policy version. Consume it and append the version and audit in one transaction. |
| Approve one clean upgrade by policy | A narrow database decision function derives eligibility from stored facts. It issues one immutable, single-attempt release approval with actor kind **policy**, policy ID/version, opt-in evidence reference, evaluation time and exact upgrade ID. Bind the candidate revision, release/root, ordered replay, installed target base, verified source pointer/fence, diff, preparation, verification evidence and capability support as in ADR-0006. The human who enabled the policy appears only on the opt-in. Only the scoped policy evaluator may invoke this function. The scheduler, verifier and AI have no approval-writing grant. |
| Approve a draft, exception or rollback | An authorized person reviews the exact revision, diff, evidence, pointer and fence under ADR-0006/0081. An explicit revert of a failed swap binds its attempt and verified predecessor. A workflow decision, provider connection, send, signature or billing decision retains its own authority. |

**Clean** means an authentic first-party successor, exact historical reproduction, complete deterministic replay of the same ordered tenant intent, and no repair, substitution or outstanding conflict anywhere in this upgrade attempt’s provenance. Candidate verification must pass for the exact root, including storage/transition compatibility, all declared projections, policy restrictions, capabilities and feature witnesses. The diff must preserve tenant bindings and effective restrictions. A wider grant, new workflow authority or external effect, destructive migration, unclassified impact, or any required human diff review or maker-checker decision makes the upgrade manual. In particular, ADR-0079 requires the owner to review a changed effective layout digest even for compatible additions. That replay can succeed without a hand merge and still require human approval. [Layout replay](ADR-0079-form-and-record-composition.md#patch-history-replay-and-reader-admission) · [Workflow authority](ADR-0073-workflow-isolate-and-authority.md#authority-boundaries)

Revocation returns the scope to Manual through a versioned human decision. Serialize issuance and the kernel’s final pre-swap check with policy changes and wave pause. Recheck authority, current policy version, candidate evidence, installed base and source fence under lock. Revocation or pause that commits first prevents an uncommitted swap, including one already approved or queued. A changed version invalidates the old approval even if the same mode is re-enabled. An already committed swap completes verification or recovery against its original evidence. Retry resumes its unique attempt. A new attempt requires a fresh eligible decision.

Keep the kernel’s compare-and-swap, pointer read-back and **independent post-swap verification**. The verifier checks either the consumed human proof or the exact policy approval and its valid opt-in at the swap, plus the committed attempt, root and fence. Only its successful receipt makes the new generation serve. Full candidate verification is available before approval. A successful post-swap receipt is evidence about what actually activated.

Before SIGNIN-1 delivers independently issued decision proofs, removes direct runtime decision writes and isolates the verifier, the opt-in is available **only in the explicitly enabled loopback local demo**, with fixture provenance in its separate store. Outside the demo the server refuses opt-in and policy approval, including direct worker calls. Demo or legacy evidence is ineligible for pilot use. [ADR-0081, pilot authority](ADR-0081-staff-sign-in-and-decision-authority.md#human-decisions-and-activation-evidence)

## From a replay conflict to a reviewable correction

First replay the source chain against its retained bases with its recorded parser, compiler profiles, evaluator bindings and acknowledgements. Compare each revision and release root. Then replay the ordered patch IDs in the sandbox against the target base at declared extension points. A compatible replay stages one child of the verified source. Historical reproduction failure, whole-package failure and an authored conflict are distinct outcomes. Only an attributable conflict names a particular tenant patch. [ADR-0013](ADR-0013-semantic-patch-lineage.md) · [Projection admission and replay](ADR-0074-formula-rule-and-workflow-projection-admission.md#release-lineage-and-replay)

Persist a versioned report against the scoped upgrade, source release/fence and target package version/root. Each attributable diagnostic carries a stable code and subject, patch ID/kind and ordered edit position, both the expected source target/contract and the observed destination target/contract (including absence). Sort findings by patch/edit order and subject. Distinguish a retired target, a moved member, an incompatible type or scope, a missing body and an unsupported format. Identify dependent edits blocked by an earlier failure. A whole-package refusal identifies the package and compiler subject. Customize and CLI status read the same report after restart. Apply field and company disclosure rules to both sides. Boot logs carry codes and opaque IDs.

For example, an edit moves **Delivery note** out of **Details**. The new base already moved it to **Shipping**. Show the expected and observed section IDs and labels the reviewer may see. The drafter may propose moving it from Shipping, or relinquishing that override. It receives only authorized terms. Save the choice as an ordinary, immutable tenant draft with a typed replacement edit linked to the refused upgrade, affected patch/edit and both base roots. Replay its explicit substitutions in order with all unaffected edits, compile, verify and show the resulting diff. Preserve the original failed attempt and edits. The release service derives correction provenance from the persisted parent and replacement edges. Redrafting, retrying or resuming the wave preserves it. An authorized person must approve **that exact new revision**. A changed base, revision, evidence or fence requires fresh review. The last verified release serves throughout.

## Rollout waves and the company version dashboard

An authorized rollout operator selects a first-party release ID/root and an ordered set of explicit tenant/environment members. Snapshot membership and target, give each member one durable upgrade identity, and record who starts, pauses and resumes each wave. Enforce uniqueness on the assignment and deduplicate by source generation and target across overlapping waves. Each member runs inside its own scope and release lock. The coordinator may enumerate assigned scope IDs and outcomes. Tenant patches, bodies and company records stay behind scoped reads and forced RLS. Wave membership grants scheduling authority only.

Start with the first wave. Stage and verify its members independently. Manual members wait for exact human approval. Conflicts and refusals keep the verified source online. Hold advancement on a refusal, conflict or failed verification and show which members need attention. An operator can pause new work in the current and later waves. Explicit resume rechecks the target, policy and source fence for each unfinished member. Advance only when the current members have verified serving receipts for their targets. Reconcile an in-flight attempt by identity after restart before starting another. Neither pausing nor resuming reverses completed activations.

The dashboard is a governed List with one row per company the viewer can read. Resolve stored company ownership and current membership before returning rows, counts or details. Show the **serving** first-party package/version and root, customization revision and verified release/fence. Alongside them show the target base, upgrade ID, wave, policy mode and status (queued, paused, pending approval, refused, conflicted, verifying or verified). A pending swap can therefore show the old serving version and the new attempt at the same time. Link to the permitted diagnostic, exact diff, recovery action and decision history. Companies sharing a pointer show the same generation. A company-only grant cannot change tenant-wide policy or activate a release for its siblings.

## Permanent CI gate for the five kinds

Build one real tenant chain through the ordinary draft, approval and activation paths. Give that tenant **all five** customizations at once. Move a first-party base with compatible additions, replay without manual edits, verify, approve by the eligible route and activate. Exercise the following behavior on the served candidate. The matrix runs on every CI change and supplies the repeatable proof from N1 onward, including workflow from N2. [Plan §12.14](../greenfield-north-star-erp-platform-plan.md#1214-breadth-proofs-before-declaring-the-north-star)

| Tenant customization | What a real request must observe after the upgrade |
| --- | --- |
| Custom field | Read back a value saved before the upgrade. Write and read another through the released form and query. |
| Validation rule | A valid invocation commits. An independently violating create or watched update returns the bound field and message before any write. Use the compiler’s pinned witnesses and live operation. [ADR-0075](ADR-0075-literal-validation-and-ordered-comparison.md) · [ADR-0076](ADR-0076-cross-field-and-to-one-validation.md) |
| Narrowed permission | The restricted role is denied at the operation gateway despite a base grant. An otherwise eligible role can still invoke it in its company. |
| Layout | In the rendered form or record, a tenant section keeps its stable ID, membership and order. Reset one independent override to base on the source. The destination’s changed default appears for that property while the retained override survives. [ADR-0079](ADR-0079-form-and-record-composition.md) |
| Workflow body | Resolve the immutable body digest, regenerate SDK bindings and verify fixtures twice with journal replay. Trigger a new occurrence under the new verified fence and observe its declared result once. A pre-upgrade run suspends at the stale fence. Its pending approval cannot authorize a new effect. [ADR-0073](ADR-0073-workflow-isolate-and-authority.md#run-pinning-replay-and-approval) |

For each kind change one destination contract independently (field collision, retired rule operand, missing role/operation, moved layout member, incompatible workflow binding). Assert the stable diagnostic and both sides before activation, across repeated attempts. Assert zero policy approvals or swaps and read the same verified source. Take one conflict through AI correction with automatic policy enabled and observe that only human approval of the corrected revision allows it to serve. On the clean path roll back by exact verified edge, with patch parsing unavailable, and read the prior release/root at the new fence, its five behaviors and retained business values.

Add a second tenant and two companies sharing a pointer. Exercise Manual, a clean policy approval, revoked opt-in, stale evidence, an in-flight pause, restart and a failed post-swap verification. Assert actual policy/human/verifier receipts, scoped dashboard results and the serving generation before and after recovery. Exercise the database entry points as the runtime and scheduler roles. Missing independent evidence must produce zero approvals, verified receipts or unauthorized pointer changes.

## Format changes and deployment

From the first external dependency on tenant data or first external login, the first-tenant ruling ends the development re-baseline. Preserve stored patch bytes, old readers, immutable manifests and business data. Every patch-format **or reader-floor** change ships a deterministic, versioned forward converter and an old-format fixture replayed through the real tenant runner. Prove original revision/root reproduction under historical semantics, then convert to an append-only derived representation linked to input digest, converter version and output digest. Reapplying the converter is idempotent. A pure floor correction can recompile the original declaration into a correctly advertised successor. Missing evidence or ambiguous semantics yields an actionable refusal. Test forward replay, verification, activation and compatible pointer rollback without a database reset. [ADR-0066](ADR-0066-pre-tenant-development-re-baselines-the-release-lineage.md) · [ADR-0074](ADR-0074-formula-rule-and-workflow-projection-admission.md)

Enforce compatibility on **every binary deployment, including downgrade and restore**. Maintain durable per-tenant/environment high-water marks for the newest patch parser and scalar surface-reader rung used. Record parser use with durable drafts, conversion or replay evidence, and the required reader with release admission/use, before acknowledging use. Seed existing stores by scanning retained patch versions and actual release features, including pending and historical releases and pinned work. Missing, inconsistent or unreadable evidence blocks admission pending reconciliation. Release rollback and policy revocation never lower either mark.

At deployment compare the candidate binary’s tested capability manifest with the fleet maxima, including offline tenant stores. Require coverage of both dimensions and every lower surface rung. Serialize capability admission with raising a mark: during a rolling deployment, only features supported by **every serving or eligible worker binary** may be used. A restored store passes the same check before it joins the fleet. Keep the fleet’s durable maxima across database restores and reject a binary with unknown capability or incomplete scope inventory. CI must use a genuine preceding reader and a downgrade attempt after pointer rollback. Assert that an incompatible binary is refused **before it can serve or process work**.

At each executable merge fetch main and allocate each new incompatible surface capability the next free scalar rung. Allocate forward migrations after the highest landed migration. Regenerate affected artifacts, schema snapshot and migration-list tests, run CI, and re-fetch before push. Renumber on collision and serialize migration merges. This proposal allocates neither number. The same gates cover calculated display fields, documents and any connector, booking or billing patch kind admitted later.
