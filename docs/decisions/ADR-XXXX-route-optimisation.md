# ADR-XXXX. Optimise routes and release them to drivers

**Proposed · ROUTES-1 slice 0 · 2026-11-21.** The owner approved the capability. The independent design read and the decisions in §9 precede implementation. Assign the ADR number at merge.

At 07:00 dispatch has an excavator delivery due by 10:00, a pickup that opens at 09:00 and a flatbed with room for only one of them at a time. The dispatcher sees the waiting jobs, tries an order, checks the remaining load and sends the reviewed run to the driver. Later, a long unload moves the rest of the day's estimates. The rentals ERP survey and `dispatch.html`, `routing.html`, `routingScheduler.js` and `googleRouteOptimization.js` supplied those workflows. ROUTES-1 implements them in Greenfield. Rain remains a separate product.

Design inputs are [PLANNER-1 at 467535a4](https://github.com/AnserBeg/2rain-greenfield/blob/467535a425eff154de8943da44e58aa2b3fc511d/docs/decisions/ADR-0090-map-resource-assignment-and-planner-workspace.md) and [ANALYZE-1 at 65bdc9f1](https://github.com/AnserBeg/2rain-greenfield/blob/65bdc9f1cd5cba4acb35e0cd049ce03c4cee7f89/docs/decisions/ADR-0090-python-analysis-and-saved-evidence.md). Both targeted reads returned REVISE. Build against their landed contracts. In particular, writable route order needs one atomic writer, and sandbox input capture must be ordered with source purge. The PLANNER-1 Geoapify recommendation still awaits sign-off.

## 1. A dispatch domain capability

The Dispatch pack owns route feasibility, custody and publication as versioned Tier B semantics under [plan §§12.3 and 12.7](../greenfield-north-star-erp-platform-plan.md#127-n4---crm-work-orders-and-their-communication). Give each record a stable ID and revision in one tenant, environment and legal entity. Procedures derive ownership from stored rows. Named Semantic Queries expose resources, waiting jobs, draft plans, published manifests and progress under live row, field and purpose grants. Named Semantic Operations carry distinct write actions and narrower state guards. Publishing a configuration registers capabilities and grants nobody access.

| Record | Facts and lifecycle |
| --- | --- |
| Truck | Vehicle or explicitly configured truck/trailer combination, eligible service class, admitted road profile, payload in whole kilograms and deck positions as integers. A driver or truck can be archived once active commitments clear. v1 does not assemble rigs or allocate trailers separately. |
| Driver | Reference to a verified staff principal, eligibility and active status. Approval names that exact person. A display name, email address or an editable profile field carries no authority. |
| Shift | One truck and one driver, finite timed duty interval, explicit start and end yard Address and optional fixed break intervals. A dispatch date groups the shift in the company zone, including an overnight shift. Time windows use ADR-0087 captures and offsets. A shift holds at most one current published route. |
| Job | Delivery, pickup or same-truck pickup-and-delivery pair. Explicit same-company source reference when admitted, direct service Address per visit, service window, service seconds, load demand and required truck class. A paired job stays whole. Source records supply facts through their governed APIs. Scheduling leaves rental, work order and inventory state under their existing owners. |
| Route | Stable route ID with immutable revisions for one shift within a draft batch. A draft may be revised, abandoned or published. Publication can supersede an unstarted manifest. Retain superseded plans and audit. The approved revision is the manifest. |
| Stop | Stable visit ID within the route, job and visit kind, position, planned service interval, before/after load and planned ETA. Yard and break endpoints are explicit route facts. Progress and updated estimates are separate recorded facts scoped to the published revision. |

Shift departure, breaks and return must fit the duty period. Require one supported road profile across selected shifts. Resolve and verify truck fit, staff eligibility and scope from records at review and commit. Company-owned shifts, yards, trucks and jobs are required in v1. Shared-resource dispatch needs a separate Booking integration. A visible empty timeline row alone confers no availability. Use overlap checks for both truck and driver in Dispatch's publication writer. The pack owns these specific commitments. [ADR-0086](ADR-0086-board-calendar-and-resource-timeline.md)

On the Planner's selected day, **Unassigned** shows open jobs with no current published assignment. The desk also shows the dispatcher's selected draft and its excluded jobs. Open the draft batch or one approved run as a Record. Group the Map by readable route revision with the truck and shift as its label. Two shifts on the same truck have distinct lines. Pin `byField` order to stored stop position, breaking ties by visit ID. Scope groups and day filters before paging. An unlocated or hidden stop follows PLANNER-1's rules for visible legs. The lines show visible stop order. Display Planned and Published as distinct views. The resource timeline reads truck and planned interval from a dispatch job projection. A paired job has one job identity in the queue and two typed visit identities in Map and timeline. Show on map seeks the exact offered visit within the current authorized day and layer.

Specify a Dispatch variant of the existing Planner binding for its day, queue, visit Map, read-only truck timeline and named draft/publish Tasks. This is a versioned binding with a reader rung. It admits the one-to-many job-to-visit projection and marks the generic assignment gesture absent. There is no new archetype or slot. Standard declared PLANNER-1 `resourceAssignment/v1` operations still handle one ordinary job relation and interval. A Dispatch job has derived assignment fields. Only the pack's draft and publication operations can stage or commit its assignment, without a generic O1 edit or a loop of job writes. For a manual move, **Edit draft** opens the pack's Task with truck, insertion position and proposed interval. The server validates and reschedules affected routes together as a new draft revision. ## 2. One input and one result contract

**Optimise routes** is an explicit Task. It takes the selected company and day, exact eligible shifts and jobs, a solver policy, and a sealed matrix reference. The server resolves all operands once in a bounded governed read snapshot and seals their identities, revisions, allowed fields, equipment eligibility, time and load constraints, published commitments, read horizon, release root and activation fence. If any required binding is hidden, any page is incomplete, or the selection changed since review, refuse capture with a remedy the person can use. Never hand a solver a truncated queue or infer values from unreadable facts. A solver sees zero ambient database, network, secret or filesystem authority. The registered host performs each necessary I/O action under that person's live grant and the named release contract.

V1 admits up to 20 eligible shifts, 100 jobs and 140 matrix nodes including every visit and explicit yard endpoint. Deduplicate nodes only when the scoped Address revision, admitted point and road profile coincide. Count node, row and byte limits after binding. Keep one finite service window per visit, fixed breaks, vehicle class, nonnegative integral load and an explicit permitted-shift set per job. Deliveries load at the start yard and reduce the carried load at the visit. Pickups add load until the end yard. Paired jobs add and then remove the same load on the same route. Test payload and deck positions at every leg, including the start load and loaded return. Both visits of a pair stay in order and are selected or omitted together. Capacity, required equipment, accessible arcs, breaks, windows and existing commitments are hard constraints.

Return a proposal with shift IDs, ordered visit IDs and explicit unplaced job IDs with bounded reason codes. Every offered job occurs exactly once as a complete placement or an unplaced entry. The domain checker computes times, wait, break placement, loads, ETA and diagnostic totals from the pinned inputs. It rejects unknown IDs, duplicate/missing jobs, split pairs, infeasible routes and malformed output before a draft can be stored. Provider and script timings carry no authority. A feasible partial draft can be reviewed and approved with the omitted jobs named. Those jobs stay in the queue. A deadline, invalid response, missing required input or unavailable provider receives an explicit outcome, creates no candidate from an unverified result and leaves any previous draft intact. **No feasible position found** describes bounded search, not proof that no solution exists.

| Level | Solver and limits |
| --- | --- |
| 1 · Built-in | Fixed-order feasible insertion. Sort jobs by configured priority, then earliest closing window and stable job ID. Score feasible insertions by added road time, then shift ID and positions. Insert both visits of a pair as a unit. Search evaluation count has a fixed versioned bound (initially 100,000 complete route evaluations). Reaching that count may return a labelled, validated partial result. Use checked integer arithmetic and a two-second monotonic wall guard. A wall timeout returns a timeout. Equal inputs, contract and algorithm version yield identical completed output. |
| 2 · Google | CONNECT-1 registers `dispatch.optimise.google/v1` with a distinct scoped installation, OAuth workload credential and disclosure grant. Use synchronous OptimizeTours and tags plus the pinned company matrix. Send request-local indices, matrix seconds, permitted-vehicle sets, load, breaks and shift/window offsets from a fixed synthetic UTC epoch. Forward no address, coordinates, contact, persistent identifier or free text. Use a maximum of 30 seconds of provider solve time, a 35-second host deadline and a 1 MiB response limit. Only send a complete finite matrix and constraints the adapter can represent exactly. Intersect visit start with closing minus service time and refuse any unsupported arc, window or break before egress. Parse all visit and skip outcomes and verify with the common checker. A returned feasible plan can vary between calls. Record the adapter version, scoped input digest, metering and admissible normalized choice. [Google model and timeouts](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/route-optimization/reference/rpc/google.maps.routeoptimization.v1) · [Timeout guidance](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/route-optimization/timeouts) |
| 3 · Company's solver | Use a published ANALYZE-1 recipe and its governed **Run analysis** Task. Its sealed inputs are the typed visits, shifts, jobs and persisted matrix. Use the admitted image and fixed input contract, within ANALYZE-1's limits of one CPU, 60 CPU-seconds, 90 monotonic seconds, 1 GiB memory and 4 MiB output. The guest has no host calls. Its isolated scratch follows ANALYZE-1. A typed suggestion holds only ordered visit aliases per shift and unplaced aliases/reasons. Save the authorized result under ANALYZE-1. Its bound **Review as route draft** Task rechecks version access and source lineage, resolves aliases to the sealed set, validates against the current Dispatch inputs and asks a human to stage the draft. It takes proposals only. Revision, source scope, capacity and approval facts come from governed server reads. A script can vary across runs. Pin the recipe, image, settings and input digest and compare actual results on repeat. |

Rank outcomes for review by jobs placed at each priority, highest first, then total travel seconds. A provider or custom solver may choose a different search strategy. Use this same summary and disclose no guarantee of optimality. Limit concurrent proposals to one per person and two per company. Validate all drafts independently of solver and revalidate at approval. A human can run the built-in option after a refused paid run. It takes a separate explicit choice.

## 3. Road travel times

Recommend a regional, operator-hosted **Valhalla** road matrix behind CONNECT-1's `dispatch.travelMatrix/v1` adapter. Pin one admitted truck costing profile, graph build, dataset date, region and adapter version. PLANNER-1's basemap and geocoder keep their own installation. Feed the road service independently captured Address pins or enrichment whose stated terms permit that use. Validate provenance, company and exact Address revision for every endpoint. The fixed private endpoint is installed by an operator and called only by the connector host. Prefer the person's explicit **Prepare travel times** choice on the proposal Task. No browser or Python reach to the service. [Valhalla matrix API](https://valhalla.github.io/valhalla/api/matrix/api-reference/)

Persist a complete immutable directed matrix through a Semantic Operation after host validation and live admission checks. Each row has typed origin/destination node IDs with stop or yard roles, origin/destination Address revisions, integer duration in seconds or explicit **Unreachable**, provider/profile/graph provenance, read horizon and contributor lineage. Only the diagonal is intrinsically zero. A missing cell or failed block fails the capture. Use a stable canonical row order. Cap one capture at 19,600 cells, 8 MiB and 10 monotonic seconds including bounded block requests, with one active capture and 20 new captures per company per UTC day. Reuse an exact scoped match with the same inputs and graph for up to 24 hours, checking rights before reuse. Quota, expiry or missing coverage yields an explicit refusal. An expired or changed source requires a new matrix to approve a new plan. Keep source and purge lineage for the run, including retention of any allowed evidence, under ADR-0078 and ANALYZE-1's capture/purge interlock.

The `dispatch.travelMatrixRows/v1` Semantic Query reads stored rows only. It applies live company, contributor row/field and analysis/export grants before emitting any durations, including derived values and saved repeats. Draft and Planner reads cannot cause a billable call. ANALYZE-1 captures the complete matrix, visits, jobs and shifts as at most four sources in its one read snapshot. At 140 nodes there are 19,600 matrix rows, leaving 400 of the runner's 20,000 aggregate rows for visits, jobs and shifts. Enforce the 8 MiB aggregate input limit and five-second capture budget too. Report the actual bound that requires a narrower selection. Road coverage and estimates are planning data. V1 has no live traffic feed or turn-by-turn navigation.

## 4. Paid requests and provider terms

The Google option defaults off. Require a scoped installation decision and a person's review of the exact disclosed inputs, provider, submitted shipment count and maximum charge. Google's global first paid tier currently lists USD 0.01 per submitted shipment for one vehicle and USD 0.03 for a fleet. A 100-job fleet request reserves USD 3 before tax. Count a paired job as one submitted shipment. Do not rely on free allowances, successful placements or volume discounts. Recommend hard limits of **USD 3 per run, USD 15 per UTC day and USD 150 per UTC month for a company**, aggregated across its installations and environments, plus one request in flight per installation and a matching provider-project quota as a backstop. The effective ceiling is the lower of this policy and the approved installation budget. Use integer tariff units and pin source, effective tariff and billing identity. Refuse an unverified or expired tariff or any over-cap reservation before egress. Reconcile external spend against the provider account under the same ledger. [Google billing units](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/route-optimization/usage-and-billing) · [Global pricing](https://developers.google.com/maps/billing-and-pricing/pricing)

Claim once through the CONNECT-1 host, recheck scope/grants, installation and fence immediately before handoff, then finish the receipt under the same bindings. Duplicate submit returns the occurrence state. Timeouts or crashes after handoff keep the full reservation counted until corroborated settlement, with no automatic retransmit or refund. A new paid attempt needs fresh human review and a new reservation. Use a separate system keyspace and an installation kill switch. Credential values exist only in scoped host slots. Audit charge, status, versions and reviewed digests without raw provider payloads. Fakes exercise admission and teardown in CI. Live requests follow CONNECT-1 and SIGNIN-1 enablement. Renaming a provider result or approving it in a Task does not settle those rights. Ship the connector behind a disabled entitlement until the exact workflow is licensed. Send no polylines and persist no raw Google response, locations, travel times or metrics. With licensing, retain only the permitted assignment and order, and compute schedule, estimates and maps from independently admitted inputs. Show provider attribution in the review as required by the applicable agreement. CI also exercises disabled and withdrawn entitlements. ## 5. Explicit approval publishes one manifest per shift

The dispatcher opens **Review and send routes** from the draft Record linked in the Planner. The Task displays each exact truck, shift and driver, revision, visit order, windows, ETA provenance, loads and unplaced jobs. It also shows effects on previously published routes, the precise recipient and the fields the driver will receive. Approving is one explicit human yes for the selected immutable batch, including any superseded unstarted manifests. It is separate from choosing a solver, paying for a run or saving a draft. The approval binds person, company, active release and activation fence, existing workflow/confirmation requirements, operation/action, draft and source revisions, matrix/profile, exact disclosure and decision digest. Editing, switching company or switching release away and back invalidates preparation.

In one Tier B Semantic Operation, under the shared Dispatch lock order, re-read stored ownership, eligibility, grants, stops, shift/duty and overlapping truck, driver and job commitments. Recompute the approved projection and hard constraints, check the active fence and consume the confirmation once. Commit the batch of route revisions and stop assignments, withdrawal of replaced driver access, audit, receipt and driver publication together. A race or changed fact refuses the whole batch with current permitted read-back. A conflicting hidden commitment produces a generic refusal with no identity or count. A lost response retries the same exact decision/key and returns its receipt under current rights. A direct API, import, workflow, assistant or generic update cannot post a manifest. The workflow host may prepare a Task under its own ceiling. [Workflow authority](ADR-0073-workflow-isolate-and-authority.md#authority-boundaries) · [Reviewed recipient and envelope](ADR-0088-governed-communications-and-reminders.md#what-is-reviewed)

**Send** means the committed manifest now appears in the assigned driver's in-app **My routes** List. This is the delivery channel for v1. Neither a draft nor a preparation creates a driver-readable row or notification. A replacement before departure requires a new yes. Its transaction swaps driver access for the new revision. An authorized dispatcher may review and withdraw an unstarted route, with audit, and its jobs return to the queue. Once the driver records departure, v1 keeps the approved order and assigned driver. A breakdown after departure is an office exception, with the last known progress and open jobs visible to dispatch. Mid-route load transfer and driver handoff need their own reviewed capability.

## 6. Arrival estimates and driver work

The checker starts with the shift's planned departure and adds directed road durations, waits until the service window opens, service time and breaks. Show planned arrival and expected service start separately where waiting occurs, with zone, offset, source and as-of time. Publish those values in the approved manifest. An authorized **Depart**, **Arrive** or **Complete stop** Task commits actuals through scoped Semantic Operations. Use server time by default and explicit reviewed corrected time if needed. Require exact current manifest and progress revision, departure before stop progress, forward stop order and nondecreasing effective actual instants. Exact retries recover a single effect. Late or duplicate progress cannot overwrite a newer estimate. Corrections append a new fact and audit.

On each committed progress change, recompute estimates for the remaining approved order from the latest authoritative actual and available scoped durations. For example, completing the unload 20 minutes late can move a pickup's estimate from 09:15 to 09:35. Surface a missed window, break or return-to-yard risk in the office. The driver may see revised estimates for that same approved route and approved fields. The approval preview declares this derivation. Record each estimate revision with source, input progress version and as-of time, separate from scheduled windows and recorded actuals. Display **Estimate unavailable** where a leg is unavailable, or **Last estimate · as of …** when inputs expire or the estimate has passed without an actual. Never retime a job, reorder stops or contact customers as an ETA side effect. A dispatcher may explicitly refresh a matching governed matrix for the remaining order. The hosted cost and permissions rules apply. Driver navigation and refresh do zero provider calls.

**My routes** uses a server-rendered List, Record and one Task per progress action, with compact ordered cards, current/next stop, authorized service Address, essential job instructions and time window. Capture scope from the live verified driver principal and the approved route revision. Apply field and company grants in List, exact get, actions and read-back, using the narrower of the approved disclosure and current rights. Other drivers, archived/superseded manifests and draft routes never qualify. A revoked or superseded link returns unavailable. Keep responses private and omit offline storage, public links, shared map tokens and attachments in v1. An ordinary dispatcher record can link a document only under the existing DOCS-1 contract. ## 9. Owner decisions for the read

1. **Can dispatch start with one company-owned truck/driver shift and limit replacements to before departure?** Recommend yes. Ship the appointment, capacity and approval loop first. Bring a loaded overnight or breakdown handoff back as a separate decision about physical custody.
2. **Which road service should supply the stored matrix?** Recommend a regional, operator-hosted Valhalla instance with a pinned dataset and truck profile. Retain the licensed Geoapify path for Planner imagery and address enrichment, subject to its separate sign-off.
3. **Should Google be enabled and at what spend?** Recommend default off, with the proposed USD 3/run, USD 15/day and USD 150/month ceilings for a licensed pilot. Obtain rights for the exact matrix input and persisted order on the non-Google Planner before any live enablement.
4. **How does a driver receive an approved route and subsequent ETAs?** Recommend My routes in the app, current assigned driver only. Show revised estimates derived from recorded progress on the same approved manifest. External delivery, customer notices and location tracking need separate decisions.

At merge assign the next available ADR number on freshly fetched main. Update this title, packet and ledger links and re-fetch before integration. Slice 0 changes documentation only.
