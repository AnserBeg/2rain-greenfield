### Declare the operation on the job type

Adopt option (b). In **Customize → Job type → Add assignment operation**, `addAssignmentOperationRevision` declares a named O1 Semantic Operation over the selected job relation and interval. The operation contract digest follows ADR-0086 ruling item 2 **exactly**. Gesture entries bind their own discriminator and typed argument roles. Keep labels, permissions, guards, confirmation, approvals and read-back out of the digest. Resolve them live.
