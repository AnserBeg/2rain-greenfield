# ADR-0084. Receivables v2 billing-origin invoice drafts

Proposed on 2026-11-20 for a separate Fable read. The BILLING-1 design ruling requests this change to Receivables. Its approval and implementation gate Billing slice 2.

## Why Receivables must own this change

Under Sales ruling C, a draft invoice names an order. Posting locks the order, refuses existing lines, and creates lines for shipped, uninvoiced quantities at their frozen prices. Payables uses the same settlement executor. An interval or usage charge has neither a shipped quantity nor an order price. The operator needs to see the exact rated lines before confirming issue. ## Proposed v2 contract

- **One origin per document.** Make the invoice/order and invoice-line/order-line relations conditional. An order-origin invoice requires its order and v1 shipment evidence. Posting still refuses pre-existing lines and derives the invoice under the order lock. A billing-origin invoice requires verified charge-set references and forbids an order relation. It may contain several services under ADR-0083’s consolidation rule. Existing v1 documents, operations and receipts retain their order semantics. Keep Payables’ spec, required relations and post-time line checks intact.
- **Persist the draft.** A Receivables draft/refresh operation accepts immutable, scoped charge-set IDs and revisions from authorized Billing generation. Resolve their customer, currency, company, terms, period and tax evidence under the fence. Write visible versioned lines and return document/line IDs and revisions. Store the authoritative rounded net, currency, first-class service-period start/exclusive end, occurrence, derivation and frozen tax evidence per rated line. Quantity and rate are explanatory. Apply platform tax to that net in cents. Refuse stale, foreign, mixed-origin or incompatible input. Protect header origin, rated lines and amounts even on a draft against generic updates or unverified pre-existing lines.
- **Entered items and credit exposure.** A named Receivables draft-adjustment operation accepts a separate positive charge with a stable ID, reason, actor, period and tax classification. Refresh preserves its provenance. A credit uses the credit operation. Serialize draft creation, refresh, adjustment, issue and void with the customer credit lock used for Sales. Include open billing drafts’ tax-inclusive amounts once in Sales and Billing exposure. Issue replaces the reservation with the posted balance. Cancel or void releases it. Incomplete exposure, an applicable hold or an exceeded/mismatched limit refuses new exposure. [Billing credit and consolidation rules](ADR-0083-period-and-usage-billing.md#who-may-write-a-draft-and-issue-it)
- **Issue the reviewed lines.** Extend the Receivables posting operation with a billing-origin path. A human gives `needsYes` against the invoice revision, origin, charge-set and tax digests, scope, release and activation fence. Recheck evidence, live grants and credit under lock. A changed line, adjustment or tax invalidates the decision. Freeze that same draft and its lines. Replays return the original receipt after authorization. Payment and per-invoice credit remain bounded by the open balance.
- **Void releases coverage.** An issued invoice can be voided only while wholly unsettled. Require an authorized confirmed operation. In one transaction mark the invoice void and release every live Billing coverage claim to a reissuable state by an append-only event. Keep original lines, receipts and coverage history. Supersede a cancelled open draft similarly. Reissue uses the original natural occurrence identity with an explicit successor allocation. A racing void, payment, generate or reissue cannot produce two live claims, double credit, or leave a service permanently consumed. A late source correction links a supplemental draft or credit to the issued line. Advance billing awaits credit-on-account or refunds.
- **Version the event.** Receivables capability v2 publishes `northstar.receivables-event/v2`. Preserve envelope, event/receipt linkage and operation type. Carry document ID, origin discriminator, invoice revision, scope, currency, affected line/occurrence references and operation-specific coverage dispositions (claimed, superseded, issued or released) for billing actions. Order events carry the order and applicable order-line references. Settlement events identify their invoice. Classify and authorize payload reads. V1 events and receipts remain decodable under their original schema. Release verification must refuse an incompatible executor, operation binding or required consumer before activation. No new event denotes a financial journal posting.

## Admission evidence

Before slice 2 writes a draft, admit the two named capability operations on one transaction and each owner’s exact write/read-back set. Exercise failure after inner writes, authorized lost-response replay, competing keys, tampered lines at issue, and void racing payment or reissue. Check actual persisted headers, lines, coverage, exposure, trust and v2 events. Run v1 order posting (including pre-existing-line refusal), v1 event replay and Payables’ existing settlement path as compatibility controls. This decision grants neither a general operation-chaining mechanism nor a ledger.
