# ADR-XXXX. Compose dashboards from governed charts and workspace panels

**Proposed for independent read · DASH-1 slice 0 · 2026-11-21.** The owner approved the capability. The grammar amendment, release vocabulary and client behaviour below are proposals. Assign the ADR number at merge.

A rental manager wants to see which yards have idle equipment, open the overdue returns and compare invoiced revenue with last month. A distributor wants to find stock-outs by depot and open the affected items. A clinic receptionist wants to see tomorrow’s appointments by site and open the bookings for one busy hour. Each can start in Customize or ask the in-app assistant. Their records, measures and vocabulary belong to their packages. The platform supplies the same parts to all three.

## 2. Draw registered chart parts from one typed result

Each part consumes the same complete, bounded authorized result for its graphic, plain-language summary, labelled HTML table and record links. Use server-drawn SVG for graphics and semantic HTML for a table panel. The renderer places marks and labels. The governed source supplies business values, period, units, comparison basis and coverage. Reject a browser chart library and arbitrary SVG, HTML, script, expressions or styles supplied by the tenant. They would add a second renderer with its own accessibility, arithmetic and trust rules. Reuse the same bar, line and stacked renderers for ADR-0089’s report outputs.

| Part and required binding | Rental yard | Distributor | Clinic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bar · category and comparable measure, visible zero baseline | overdue returns by yard | back orders by depot | appointments by site |
| Line · ordered time or numeric dimension and measure, break at absent observations | daily units on hire | weekly units shipped | daily visits |
| Area · ordered dimension and one nonnegative measure against zero | occupied machine-hours over time | occupied pallet spaces by day | booked chair-hours by day |
| Stacked bar · category, disjoint series and additive measure in one partition | hired hours by equipment class per yard | shipped units by service level per depot | visits by appointment type per site |

Separate currencies and incompatible units visibly in every view, including the ADR-0089 **No currency** partition. Use exact source values for labels, tables and comparison arithmetic. Normalize only drawing coordinates. A comparison binds the same measure, partition, company and non-date filters to two named windows. Display their dates and basis. Distinguish a full previous month from a previous month-to-date. Require the source’s historical semantics. A missing observation leaves a gap. A zero prior value makes a relative change unavailable. A zero whole has no donut slices. Label an out-of-range gauge value and show its exact value. Refuse negative parts, overlapping shares or stage snapshots presented as conversions. Ratios and funnel cohorts need a declared measure with resolvable contributors. A stock balance cannot acquire a history by being plotted on a time axis. These checks prevent a plausible-looking chart from changing the meaning of the data. [Report arithmetic and dates](ADR-0089-declared-reports.md)

Give the figure an accessible name and description. Identify series with labels plus patterns, glyphs or line styles. Label axes, period, units and coverage. Every mark offers the same named drill as its table cell, including each stack segment, heat cell, spark point and funnel stage. Lines and areas drill at observed points. A KPI’s comparison link distinguishes both source periods. The gauge links the observation, with any authored target labelled as a reference. Tables provide one predictable keyboard path to every mark and a labelled drill target of at least 44 px when marks are dense. Touch, keyboard, screen readers and script-off operation can all reach the same values and records. Render an empty result with its meaning and a filter action. Use authorized, escaped text and platform-owned SVG primitives and references only. [Accessibility and component contracts](../greenfield-north-star-erp-platform-plan.md#83-component-registry)

## 3. Give every number and drill the viewer’s authority

Do not reveal hidden members as empty legend entries. Exhausted work says **Narrow the filter**. An unavailable or incomplete total cannot become a zero, including in a sparkline.

## 7. Give a dashboard one measured read budget

Proposed v1 admission limits are **8 panels, 4 distinct governed data requests after deduplication, 200 plotted data marks in total and 2 MiB of rendered markup**. Count both periods, child views and evidence eligibility in the plan. Enforce the tighter of page, query and report limits. Charge the complete input probes, groups and bytes across the request, including batched statements and retries. Use ADR-0089’s 10,000 candidate facts, 500 groups and five-second monotonic deadline as dashboard-wide ceilings for report work. Reserve capacity before issuing each batch. Never sample or truncate a complete measure to fit. Offer **Narrow the filter** for data overflow, and fewer panels or distinct sources for an over-budget design. The plain table observes the same completeness limit. Admitted native child pages keep their own explicitly labelled coverage.

Batch compatible source reads with typed parameters inside one SPEED-1 read-only request snapshot. Key memoization by the actual principal, tenant/environment/company, release/fence, policy epoch, read instant, source contract and normalized filters, projection, partition, bounds and drill semantics. Share only identical authorized results. Discard at request end. Recheck live epoch and serving fence before releasing bytes. On change discard the entire response and follow the gateway’s bounded retry/refusal. Personalized HTML stays `no-store`. An adapter requiring an out-of-snapshot cache write must gain a governed joinable read before it can supply a dashboard. Reject independent per-tile budgets and cross-request data caches because they multiply database work and can retain revoked access. [SPEED-1 request meter](../execution/packets/SPEED-1.md) · [Read authorization](ADR-0071-read-decisions-are-live-per-request-writes-per-call.md)

Meter the served warm page **including the shell** at ≤2 DB transactions, ≤40 statements, ≤3 policy reads, zero identical repeat statements, zero warm manifest/query/operation catalog parses and at most one binding parse per distinct bound surface. Measure p95 against the 400 ms registered-query target. Cover sparse and full eight-panel fixtures, shared vs distinct reads, both periods, cap and cap-plus-one, a policy change and a failed proof of evidence. Count actual DB work and bytes, including attributable child loads, so moving work into a fragment or image request cannot evade the budget. Account for governed evidence byte delivery and map-provider work under the child’s own authority and ceilings as well. Record cold paths separately. If the whole-page target cannot be met, bring the measured shape back before shipping it.

## 8. Keep chart interaction in the document

Apply ADR-0036 §2a separately to each interaction. The proposed amendments grant only the behaviours described below once their prerequisites and gates exist. They add no script source, general fetch hook or chart runtime. PLANNER-1’s map exception belongs to that child alone. | Interaction and decision | 1 · Script domain | 2 · Native alternative and its limit | 3 · Required capability | 4 · Gate on the served page |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Exact-value tooltip · conditionally admit one behaviour | The owned script may show an already-rendered, escaped label for one offered mark on focus or hover, keep it open while hovered and dismiss it on Escape, blur or leaving both. | The visible table and inline focus label work with script off. Native SVG titles cannot guarantee a keyboard-invoked, hoverable, Escape-dismissible popover. | First ship server-labelled marks, table links and focus return. No lookup, chart geometry, data cache or hidden value in the DOM. | Focus, hover and dismiss, keep focus on the mark, reach the same value by touch and with JS off. Inspect a withheld result for text and data attributes. |
| Highlight · admit native styles | Local `:hover` and `:focus-visible` outline the offered mark. | Owned CSS delivers the whole interaction. A new script behaviour fails the native-necessity test. | Labelled SVG marks, patterned series, Ledger focus tokens and the table equivalent. | Compare pointer and keyboard contrast in both themes with JS off. A highlight neither selects records nor changes a filter. |
| Apply filters in place · conditionally extend behaviour seven | Submit the workspace’s own same-origin read form and replace its one server-rendered dashboard region, including every panel and its applied filter summary, atomically. | Native GET offers complete URL-based navigation. It cannot retain surrounding shell DOM, focus and scroll while refreshing just that region. | Ship the GET path and page-wide read budget first. Heavy animation, live drag-rearranging and streaming charts are later, separate §2a amendment candidates. Each needs a user task the current controls cannot deliver, its own data and authority model where necessary, and an observable fallback and gate.
