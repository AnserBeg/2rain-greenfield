# ADR-0083. Period and usage billing

Proposed on 2026-11-20 for the orchestrator’s Fable read. Slice 0 is design only.

A customer should be able to price the use of a room, a service contract, a subscription or a metered item with the same capability. Billing owns the calculation. The source module owns the service facts. Receivables owns the invoice. Each can change independently because a charge identifies the exact versions of all three contracts that produced it.

Ship Billing as a versioned Tier B domain pack at customization ladder rung 6. It contributes commercial pricing at N3 through the existing release compiler, Q2 Semantic Queries and O2 Semantic Operations. An issued invoice is an operational customer document. It creates no journal entry, revenue recognition, inventory movement, cost relief or accounting balance. A future N6 ledger pack must separately admit posting, periods, reversal and reconciliation before it can treat an invoice as an accounting input. ## Which service is billable

A binding identifies a persisted record or owned line by stable type and ID. Its declared Semantic Queries return the commercial owner, customer, company, item if applicable, and a stable service identity shared by its intervals and usage observations. A source must provide an interval, metered usage or both. A usage-only service can group many log entries for one allowance. Two bindings to the same service and charge component cannot create two billing entitlements. Persist the service’s billing anchor once so that a form rename, reassignment of equipment, retry or release change cannot start another clock.

| Source evidence | Meaning at rating time |
| --- | --- |
| Interval | Half-open start and end instants, quantity and unit, with an explicit agreed or actual basis. Clip open actual use and open pauses to the captured as-of instant. An absent return does not authorize billing future actual time. |
| Booking | Consume the authorized owner-detail projection of agreed interval, actual use, quantity, assignment legs, pauses and event revisions proposed by BOOKING-1. Availability and free/busy disclose too little to bill. A swap preserves service identity. A hold or cancellation alone is insufficient evidence of service. Advance billing needs a confirmed agreement. [Booking interval and history contract](https://github.com/AnserBeg/2rain-greenfield/blob/ea1515aeb3c8dba2f9d554223108a63a2e9af417/docs/decisions/ADR-0082-booking-over-time.md) |
| Meter readings | Stable stream, unit, event ID/revision, instant and nonnegative exact cumulative value. Difference consecutive readings at required boundaries. Refuse a missing baseline, decrease/reset, conflicting observations or a gap across a required split. Ask for a corrected or boundary reading through the source operation. |
| Usage entries | Stable event IDs/revisions and nonnegative quantities in a declared unit. Attribute each increment at its effective instant. An entry exactly at the exclusive end belongs to the next window. Overlapping or ambiguous interval usage requires an exact split supplied by the source. |

A subscription period and a service-plan line use the same interval contract. Neither requires Booking or Inventory. The compiler resolves query IDs, field types, units, owner/line relations, read classifications and required permissions. Bind the queries for customer/list selection and calendar evidence as well. Execute only the declared queries under the acting principal, release, tenant, environment and stored company scope. A write consumes a complete versioned snapshot under the same transaction or verifies an equivalent source revision fence, including inserted or removed children. The source query must attest completeness for the specific service and horizon. A policy-filtered list cannot certify that no pauses exist. Partial visibility, truncated results, conflicting ownership or stale revisions refuse the whole draft. A read of a shared item or customer grants no access to another company’s charges.

## The rate plan a business user publishes

A plan has a stable ID and immutable, effective-dated versions. Customize asks for a currency, terms below, and a billing schedule. The compiler admits only named, typed settings. Unknown members or versions, duplicate component IDs, negative terms, impossible minimum/cap combinations and unsupported bounds fail with a diagnostic at the setting. Omitted optional components contribute nothing. At least one priced component is required. The tenant supplies data and selects a policy. The platform supplies the complete algorithm. There is no executable text, tenant loop, expression callback, ambient clock or unbounded traversal. [Expression ceiling](ADR-0012-expression-kernel-ceiling.md)

| Setting | Version-one decision and reason |
| --- | --- |
| Time rates | At most one nonnegative exact price per hour, day, week, four weeks and month, per service unit. A fixed day is 24 elapsed hours, a week 7 such days and four weeks 28. A month uses the actual elapsed span to the corresponding local date and time next month, clamped at month end. Compute these lengths once at the start of the pricing window so the chosen combination is reproducible. |
| Minimum and cap | Optional minimum duration and minimum money per unit for positive active time, once per pricing window. An optional ceiling applies to the time subtotal per unit in each successive, non-overlapping cap period anchored at service start. Select the period and a money amount or a named period rate, such as the four-week rate. A cap establishes that period as the time pricing window, independent of invoice dates. Without a cap, the invoice cycle is the time pricing window. The form shows both the reset date and which components the ceiling covers. |
| Partial time | Select whole started units (default) or prorate the last fraction of the shortest offered unit. Longer offers can always cover the tail at their full price. This allows a week to beat six individual days without making a single day cost one seventh of every weekly offer. |
| Pauses | Subtract the union of paused intersections before measuring time. An entirely paused window has zero time charge, including zero minimum. Pauses also suspend recurring fees. Meter usage during a pause is excluded, requiring boundary readings or separately timed increments. |
| Meter and fixed fee | A named meter component binds one stream/unit, an included quantity per service per invoice cycle (default zero) and an exact money rate per excess unit. Unused allowance expires at the cycle boundary. A named fixed component charges its stated amount once per service per cycle. Each has a distinct line and tax classification. The fee and the included quantity prorate for active service coverage. The overage unit price stays fixed. |
| Overrides | Select one complete, compatible rate table per pricing window. A customer override wins, then the highest-priority applicable active price-list override assigned to that customer in the invoice currency, then the plan base. An item attachment takes precedence over a type default. Equal-priority competing matches refuse. Snapshot the winner, assignment evidence and version. Whole-table selection prevents unintentionally combining one customer’s minimum with another list’s cap. Catalog item unit prices and quantity breaks already serve sales order entry. A billing override explicitly prices the period or usage unit. |
| Currency and scale | Tag money with one supported invoice currency (initially CAD, USD or EUR), usage with a unit, and decimals with precision and scale. Compile every rate, cap reference, override, meter and invoice mapping against those types. Mixed currencies, incompatible units, undeclared currency or unrepresentable bounds refuse compilation. A dynamic source selection must match its compiled currency case again at runtime. No FX conversion. |

Pin currency, service identity, calendar anchor and units at enrollment. Changing one after use requires closing that enrollment and explicitly continuing at a non-overlapping cutover. A price change appends a version prospectively with an explicit effective instant. Opening a late historical cycle resolves the version in force at its boundary. Select it at the first unopened pricing window for time and at the first unopened invoice cycle for fixed fees and meters. An open component retains its pinned table and assignment. Changes to schedule require a reviewed prospective cutover. Labels alone may change freely. Persist every version needed to interpret drafts and issued facts. ## How a line earns its amount

1. Pin one calculation version, plan version, resolved override, currency, calendar boundaries and source snapshot. Intersect eligible service with the priced window. Union intervals and pauses before subtraction so overlapping pauses and adjoining assignment legs cannot duplicate time. Rate each separately identified commercial quantity once. A change in quantity requires disjoint, versioned quantity evidence and an explicit allocation of the prior entitlement. Refuse an ambiguous change.
2. For positive active time, take the greater of its duration and the time minimum. Find the lowest exact cost that covers that duration using the offered periods. A bounded implementation can enumerate counts of each longer period up to the count that alone covers the target, then fill the uncovered tail with the shortest period under the partial-time setting. Include cover by a single long period and by mixed periods. Prune dominated covers without changing the result. Break exact ties by fewer blocks and then by counts in descending order of period length, preferring more of the first differing period (month first on equal length). Apply the money minimum, then the cap, then multiply by the service quantity. A time pricing window is at most a calendar month or four weeks, and a minimum duration must fit its window. This bounds the search independently of service lifetime. Equal-length offers compete on price, then stable ID.
3. For a cycle, divide active service duration by its full resolved duration. Multiply the recurring amount and the allowance by this exact fraction. For a usage-only enrollment, use its effective coverage dates for the fraction, so zero reported consumption still has a defined allowance. For each stream compute `max(0, eligible usage − prorated allowance) × overage rate` once across the service. A fixed fee or overage sits outside the time ceiling. A missing required reading is an incomplete calculation.
4. Use integer coefficients for decimals and exact rational numerators/denominators for time fractions. Compare unrounded candidates. Round the final net amount of each time, fee or overage line half-up to invoice cents. Let the platform’s commercial arithmetic apply the frozen tax rate to the rounded line and sum rounded lines. Explicitly untaxed is a valid tax decision. An unreadable or unresolved required tax rate refuses. Pin the input precision and product bounds against the receiving invoice fields. Enforce versioned numeric, source-cardinality, window and candidate limits before mutation. Limit exhaustion returns a typed refusal without a partial price. [Commercial arithmetic](../../packages/postgres-provider/src/commercial-amounts.ts)
5. Attach the derivation to each line. Retain scoped source IDs/revisions and recoverable snapshots, agreed/actual basis, original and excluded spans, units, cycle and pricing window, plan/override IDs, rate counts, exact arithmetic before and after minimum and cap, allowance and usage, proration fraction, rounding, tax evidence and prior billed amount. Store the canonical input and result digests with the algorithm and release versions. The explanation query shows the person the winning terms and the arithmetic that affected that line, subject to the source and invoice read permissions.

For example, at CAD 30 per day and CAD 100 per week, ten days with one paused day cost CAD 160 for one unit (one week and two days). With a CAD 240 ceiling per service-anchored four weeks, twenty active days in that window cost CAD 240. Publishing a different weekly rate later leaves both derivations reproducible. On a separate CAD 31 monthly subscription with 10 included units, half a cycle active and 8 eligible units at CAD 2 overage produce two net lines, CAD 15.50 and CAD 6.00.

## When a draft is due

Choose monthly calendar (first business date of each month) or monthly anniversary (the agreed anchor day). Resolve boundaries in the pinned tenant business calendar and IANA zone. Clamp the original anniversary independently each month, so January 31 goes to February’s last day then March 31. Keep resolved instants and offsets, reject a missing or ambiguous boundary until explicitly resolved, and use actual elapsed duration across DST for proration. A calendar cycle and a four-week rate can have different ends.

**Cutover** records the first billable instant and the last covered boundary from the prior process, with an authorized operator’s evidence of what has already been charged. Start inclusively at the first unbilled instant and preserve the original cycle/price anchors. An opening partial cycle requires cumulative amounts, cap used, allowance used and a boundary reading where applicable. Refuse unknown prior coverage or overlapping enrollments. Starting at a clean boundary gives zero opening amounts. Persist coverage even for zero charges so a retry cannot resurrect a paused period.

**Arrears** prices actual evidence through the cycle’s exclusive end. **Advance** may draft agreed coverage and recurring fees at the cycle start from confirmed facts. Meter overage always waits for actual usage. At close, reconcile advance time and fees against the bound source of delivered service and pauses under the pinned terms. For a subscription this can be an effective, non-cancelled contract period. For actual-use bookings it requires actual use or an authoritative cancellation. Missing evidence leaves the cycle visibly pending. For a pricing window spanning invoices, calculate the cumulative rounded entitlement through each boundary and subtract amounts already allocated to that window. Carry its consumed minimum/cap across the boundary. This also assigns a fractional cent exactly once. A due run consumes canonical cycles in order. Arbitrary user date ranges can preview, but cannot reset a minimum, cap or allowance.

While a draft is open, an explicit refresh supersedes its revision and requires a new review. After issue, any difference becomes a linked supplemental draft or a credit proposal through Receivables, using the original terms and tax evidence for the service corrected. A previously settled invoice can exceed today’s creditable balance under the present credit operation. Preserve the full difference as an open, visible exception until a supported Receivables remedy can settle it. Never discard it, silently net it against another customer or rewrite an issued line. Cancellation, void and reissue retain history and require a named operation to release or reassign the original coverage.

## Who may write a draft and issue it

Today the Sales Task creates an order-linked draft and calls `customer_invoice_post`. Receivables locks the order, requires shipped uninvoiced quantity, refuses pre-existing invoice lines and creates the lines at posting. Payments, credits and voids operate on that customer invoice. General service billing needs an explicit new input to this flow. [Sales Task](../../packages/domain/src/sales/workspace.ts) · [Receivables operations](../../packages/postgres-provider/src/receivables-capability-executor.ts) · [Settlement writer](../../packages/postgres-provider/src/settlement-capability-executor.ts)

- Extend the invoice owner’s operations with a typed billing origin alongside the existing order origin. The Receivables draft operation accepts a verified immutable charge-set reference, resolves its scope, customer, terms, tax classification and currency, creates or refreshes the draft invoice and its visible draft lines, and returns their IDs/revisions. Exactly one origin applies. The existing shipment route keeps its order, shipment and uninvoiced-quantity checks. A booking or subscription needs no artificial order, item movement or shipment.
- **Generate charges** takes service/cycle references, expected revisions and an idempotency key. The authorized operation obtains the declared source facts and invokes the invoice owner’s governed draft operation with its own authorization and bound receipt. The charge set, natural coverage claim, invoice draft lines, links, audit, change, outbox and receipts must commit together or all roll back. Implement the operation composition on a shared transaction in slice 2. Billing has no invoice-table grant. Read back the actual rows and amounts before commit. A partial failure cannot consume coverage and lose the invoice.
- Serialize competing generation, refresh, adjustment and issue on stored service/window/component and invoice identities in a common order. Enforce uniqueness in storage on tenant/environment/company, durable service, component and canonical coverage occurrence. Preserve it across release, plan versions, invoice IDs and request keys. Allocate each source event once within its stream. Check live authority before replay. Same actor, operation and canonical input under the same key return the original receipt. A different payload under that key refuses. A fresh key for already covered work returns its existing effect or an explicit revision conflict. Only a named adjustment can add the difference for corrected evidence. [Tier B writer pattern](../architecture/posting-kernel-guarantees.md)
- **Issue invoice** is a separate, confirmation-class (`needsYes`) human decision at the Receivables posting operation. Show the customer, dates, lines, derivations, tax and total. Bind confirmation to the exact invoice revision, charge and tax snapshot, company, release and activation fence. Recheck the human’s live scope/grant and all relevant revisions under lock. Changed material evidence requires refresh and fresh confirmation. On success freeze the lines and mark the same draft issued through the normal invoice lifecycle. No tax guessing, automatic issue or outward send occurs on Generate.
- Each capability version publishes its authoritative read/write dependency set, including traversed query fields, calendar, scope, plan and assignments, service evidence, coverage, draft/issued provenance and tax inputs/outputs. Generate its only storage facade from the set. Check indirect reads and operation effects, ban raw SQL in the capability package, and review each declared fact for necessity. The compiler refuses tenant defaults, derived displays, rules, imports, generic CRUD or workflow effects that produce those facts outside the owning operations. Authorized input entry and tenant guards or validations that narrow admission remain available. In a declared, authorized recovery invocation evaluate tenant narrowing as report-only and audit it. Platform isolation, integrity, tax and audit checks remain mandatory. [Rule admission](ADR-0074-formula-rule-and-workflow-projection-admission.md) · [Plan §10.6](../greenfield-north-star-erp-platform-plan.md)

WORKFLOW-1 timers may wake on a persisted cycle boundary, query due work, and prepare the exact Generate action. A wake confers no authority. Before N5 a Tier B workflow write requires human approval of that exact plan, including when it only drafts. Dispatch rechecks actor, workflow grants, scope, release and fence. Replayed or late wakes use the original occurrence. Issuing always requires its own human decision. [Workflow authority](ADR-0073-workflow-isolate-and-authority.md)

## Compose it in Customize

An author selects **Attach rate plan** on a record type or item, chooses the rates, and marks a record or line **Billable** from an interval, booking or meter query. The form binds **Generate charges** and **Issue invoice** to their released operations and explanations. The same bindings serve the assistant. The proposed add-only patch members add a plan version, attach the plan, and add a billable-source binding. Each member has a stable ID, a closed payload and a `schemaVersion`. Resolve references in dependency order inside one bounded, atomic change set. Reject changed ID reuse, dangling references, unsupported query shapes or attempted relaxation of confirmation.

For “equipment rental with daily, weekly and monthly rates and a 28-day cap,” the AI drafter builds one bundle with the record types, BOOKING-1 bookable resource and owned line, rate plan, attachment, billable booking projection and the two invoice actions. Ask for missing amounts, currency or cap basis before approval. A subscription uses the same members with a service period or meter stream. Both go through the ordinary plain diff, compile, verification, authorized approval, activation and rollback. No example-specific dispatch enters platform code.

Each new capability that an older reader must refuse takes the next scalar rung on freshly fetched main **at its executable merge**. Regenerate artifacts, check a real preceding reader refuses, reproduce omitted-feature release bytes for first-party and tenant patches, and re-fetch before pushing. Patch replay must reproduce the original root and refuse incompatible rebinding. Rollback changes the active release pointer while preserving issued lines, open drafts, coverage and historical readers. Surface stranded drafts before rollback and require a compatible active binding to resume. Deployments must honor the highest patch parser and surface-reader floors already used by any tenant.

## Slices after the design ruling

1. **Quote a service.** Publish typed plans and the deterministic calculator/explanation query. Property-test best price against exhaustive small covers, cap and minimum order, arbitrary pause overlaps, month boundaries, proration and half-cent ties. Check input permutations, bounds, mixed-currency refusal and historical version replay.
2. **Draft a real invoice from time and usage.** Ship governed source queries, booking/meter bindings, atomic Receivables draft operations and separate human issue. Race two keys and a lost response against one occurrence. Inspect the actual invoice, coverage and trust rows after each commit or injected failure. Deny a cross-company reference and a stale confirmation. Run the original shipped-order journey alongside it.
3. **Run the billing cycle.** Add cutover, advance/arrears reconciliation and WORKFLOW-1 timers. Test a Jan 31 anniversary, DST, a pause spanning a cycle, a cap spanning two invoices, a late reading, restart after commit, revocation before dispatch and the same wake delivered twice. Compare cumulative billed cents with the final entitlement.
4. **Compose or ask.** Ship the Customize bindings, grouped AI draft and both actions using the same patch compiler. Exercise review through activation, real prior-reader refusal, upgrade replay and rollback with an open draft.
5. **Prove reuse.** From clean tenants generate an equipment-hire module and a subscription module using customization only. In each, enter service, generate, inspect and issue through the normal UI. Change the plan and reproduce the first invoice. Require zero module-specific source code, migrations or platform branches for either proof.
