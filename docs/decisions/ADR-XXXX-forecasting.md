A controller wants to know whether payroll in week three takes the company below its cash buffer. A buyer wants to know which yard will run out of a part before a confirmed receipt. A rental manager wants to compare the capacity already committed next week with what the fleet can serve. In **Customize → Forecasts** an authorized owner chooses a supplied method, reviews its sources and defaults, and publishes it. Staff run it from the declared company surface and can see which assumptions produced the estimate.

**Cash coverage (A2).** Show the current business-day opening cut. Every source must reconstruct facts recorded by that instant. Refresh times may differ if their coverage and reconciliation establish the same cut. The governed cash pool fixes the company, currency and effective account membership. The obligation source identifies posted AR/AP due to settle through that pool using versioned authoritative routing and amount allocations. Exclude obligations with proven routing elsewhere. Withhold when the scope, a required allocation, currency or due date is unresolved. A posted payable and its associated forecast payroll or recurring occurrence count once, as the payable. Withhold an unresolved overlap until the operator excludes the estimate or the source proves its association.

Use effective instants before opening for the book opening. A known cash movement effective exactly at opening belongs to the first forecast date as a known movement. A cash movement effective later or recorded after the cut contributes neither to opening nor to settlement of opening residuals. Reconcile opening book balances to governed movements by account and currency. Match cash-backed obligation settlements to stable movement and allocation IDs, amounts and direction. A movement can be split across obligations only up to its proven amount. Identify the unapplied remainder and prove either its allocation to a scoped obligation or its treatment as cash with no overlapping forecast receipt or payment. Within the selected pool count each movement once. A settled amount from a proven outside-pool account reduces the obligation without adding pool cash. Reduce projected residuals by matched partial cash allocations and effective posted noncash credits or write-offs. Credits and write-offs never add cash. Match reversals to the facts they reverse at the cut. Opening-side cash is already in the balance. Boundary-side cash appears once on the first day and reduces the matching expected residual. An unresolved movement, allocation, reversal or possible overlap withholds the affected projection, including when both source watermarks cover the same cut.

For example, a $50,000 opening plus a $12,000 receivable, less $8,000 bills and $20,000 payroll, closes at $34,000. At another cut the $62,000 opening already includes the $12,000 deposit. With a proven application or pending allocation of the full deposit to that invoice, expected receipts for it are zero and the close is still $34,000. With the same cut and no proof of allocation or disjointness, withhold the projection instead of showing $46,000. If the deposit is effective exactly at opening and already known, show $50,000 opening and $12,000 first-day known cash, with zero expected for the matched invoice. Changing the payroll date on the same facts moves the dip in a new scenario.

**Stock coverage.** Score one supported stock key at a time in v1. Refuse unresolved locations, base-unit mismatches, unknown dates for scoped commitments/inbound, and incomplete history. A confirmed overdue outbound remainder is due on the first day. An overdue inbound promise requires an updated expected date. Forecast 28 days by default (7–91 days). Show projected shortfall and at-risk dates as daily closing estimates. Label the history “fulfilled demand”. Lost sales and substitution are outside the model. With 18 units on hand, a day-one baseline of six and eight due gives a close of 10. A day-two baseline of six and seven confirmed inbound brings the close to 11. Starting from available stock or adding commitments to baseline would count claims twice. Pooling yards would hide local shortages. [ADR-0016](ADR-0016-stock-identity-dimension-set.md)

**Rental coverage (A4).** Start with serialized, company-owned fleets and confirmed bookings. Run 28 days by default (7–91 days). For each physical unit subtract the union of maintenance and off-hire blocks from its fleet schedule. Merge booking and possession for the same claim, then union claims across all rentals and intersect with the rentable intervals for served hours. Show excess simultaneous claim hours and the portions that maintenance or another customer’s possession prevents the unit from serving as conflict or unserved commitment hours. Count each unserved claim-hour once even when several causes apply. Current possession takes precedence over a later booking. A declared future return can be moved by the reviewed delay. An open leg with a missing or already reached expected return has **Unknown return** status. Flag the occupied unit as unavailable for a promised next assignment through the remaining window until a governed return estimate or actual return is recorded. Show the exposure and overlapping commitments as at risk without treating the unknown interval as evidenced served hours. Unassigned demand is visible as unresolved pressure without served hours. Quote conversion, shared pools and re-rent are outside v1. A four-unit, 24-hour day with one unit down all day and 36 occupied unit-hours yields 36/72, or 50%. [ADR-0082](ADR-0082-booking-over-time.md)

**Time and values.** Start at the working company’s captured business-day boundary. Use half-open intervals and adjacent civil dates, with 13 consecutive seven-date buckets from that boundary for cash. Resolve each boundary in its recorded IANA zone, applying the admitted gap/overlap rule and recording calendar, rule and tzdb pins. Rental interval weights come from elapsed time between the resolved instants. Cash daily lows make no claim about intraday ordering. Use signed exact decimal money and stock units, exact ratios for utilisation and model averages, and an explicit display rounding rule. Hold the scope, unit and method fixed in each comparison. Browser time and fixed 24-hour shifts can place a fact in the wrong date. Floating-point currency can introduce residual differences. ## 2. Give each set of assumptions a version people can compare

**Customize** publishes one add-only `addForecastRevision` member with a closed, versioned union for the three calculation kinds. Use stable forecast, assumption and revision IDs, an expected predecessor, a chosen method/version, typed query/field selections with scope/grain/time/unit contracts, an authoritative company-record binding, an allowed output surface binding, an optional governed Task binding and a default scenario. Defaults are explicit and reviewable. Active/retired is a closed variant; a retired revision preserves identity and carries no live bindings. The compiler resolves IDs, types, ranges, precision, source purpose and declared company scope. Labels resolve at render time. First-party starter settings and in-app authors use this same path.

A company operator uses a **Run forecast** Task to choose eligible inputs and edit an allowed scenario privately. The shape includes typed offsets, thresholds, lookback and horizon, and cash cost lines with currency supplied by the cash pool and an explicit one-time or finite recurrence rule. Cash cost occurrences use nonnegative amounts, effective date bounds and stable IDs. A recurring run expands only dates in the displayed horizon. The Task previews normalized values, sources, skipped optional inputs and what changed from the published defaults. Pin a run to the exact scenario values and digest. Changing a scenario produces a new run, while making it the company default requires a new reviewed release revision through Customize. Each company may keep several named forecast definitions. Customize uses the actual company record and its publication grant, and the compiler prohibits cross-company source or surface bindings. This leaves Tuesday’s forecast legible after Friday’s assumptions change. Global settings would overwrite the context of existing forecasts. Free-form formulas or learned defaults would obscure what the owner approved. [ADR-0079](ADR-0079-form-and-record-composition.md)

**Assumption protection and comparison (A6).** Immutable default values, including payroll dates, amounts and recurrence details, are protected company-scoped definition fields. The shared release pins the protected revision and its exact approved digest. Shared catalog and schema projections expose neither protected values nor derived hashes, hints or diffs that disclose them. The definition reader requires live company and field/classification grants for every protected value it serves, including Customize preview, diff, revision history, Task defaults, and AI drafting context. Publishing and exact candidate approval require access to the impacted fields and the company publication grant. Authoring permission alone grants neither. Trial inputs, changes and evidence are visible only to the trial author under current grants. For an assumptions-only comparison, calculate both variants in one run against the same authorized captured facts, cut, method and scope. Both variants share the aggregate limits. For separately captured results compare their source digests and cuts, and mark differences in facts or coverage visibly before showing any delta. Such a delta cannot be attributed to the assumption alone.

## 3. Capture once, publish once, reauthorize each read

**Population authority (A1).** The gateway supplies the acting person, active company, tenant/environment and verified release root/fence. Resolve entity ownership and scope from persisted records. Each method declares its population by company, scope key, time window and source role, including optional roles when selected, contributors, joins, filters, grouping and classification dependencies. Before reading source rows, prove from declared scope and live grants that the person can read every possible member of every required role, with all required fields. A scoped grant that covers the whole declared population can pass. If row policy cannot be proved to cover that population from grants and scope alone, withhold. Request parameters may select a declared authorized population. Caller filters, visible contributor lists, caller-filtered totals and a “Restricted” aggregate cannot prove completeness.

Apply company, row, field and classification policy in the provider before aggregation. Read sources and contributor lineage in one bounded read-only snapshot at a comparable cut, retaining canonical query IDs/contracts, normalized inputs, horizons and contributor IDs and revisions. Discovery omits an unauthorized choice. Direct run, list, detail, saved evidence and handoff use the same policy and return the existing generic unavailable response or discovery omission. Those responses disclose no values, source or fallback labels, sample or omission counts, exception text or other metadata. They are identical for the same grants whether the restricted subset is empty or populated. Coverage and freshness explanations are available only after population authorization. An optional fallback is for an authorized source with proved insufficient coverage or sample size, or an unconfigured optional role. Denying a configured role cannot select a fallback. Connector-fed facts qualify through admitted stored queries with complete watermarks. [ADR-0071](ADR-0071-read-decisions-are-live-per-request-writes-per-call.md) · [ADR-0080](ADR-0080-connector-framework.md) · [ADR-0089](ADR-0089-declared-reports.md)

Proposed standard-run ceilings are 10,000 source facts, 8 MiB of canonical inputs, 4 MiB of lineage, 500 output rows and 2 MiB of output per run, with stricter source scan limits and a five-second monotonic capture deadline. Backtests have the aggregate cap in §5. Bound visited rows and bytes at the gateway, including a cap-plus-one probe, and refuse the whole run on excess. Allow one active run per person and at most two per tenant/environment, with a lower limit when host capacity requires. Cap pending runs at four per tenant/environment and expire them after a two-minute wait. Reserve the slot and bytes atomically before work. Every read or publication recheck has a five-second monotonic budget. Enforce a 30-second execution deadline, cancel queued and running work, and check cancellation, live policy and the serving fence before handing off a capture or publishing a result. An operator or tenant stop prevents new starts and late publication. A worker cannot start as a privileged substitute for the person. Truncation could silently skew the forecast. Unbounded scans or queues let one company stall other work.

An immutable **Forecast estimate** records kind, scope, actor, release/fence, method version, assumption revision and exact values, query contracts and watermarks, input and output digests, coverage and exclusions, and typed period rows and summary. It is available through its admitted evidence List and Record. An explicit idempotent **Save forecast** can retain one private version and audit event from an authorized completed run. Recheck current population and assumption authority and stored contributor lineage for every run, list, search, detail, page or controlled handoff, including archived source reads where allowed. Use one scoped set-based check per source after the population proof under a request budget. Exhaustion or a purged/unverifiable contributor withholds the version and its metadata. Recheck before release of bytes. Saved evidence lives in company-scoped provider stores (`forecast_runs`, `forecast_run_lineage` and immutable `forecast_versions`), with no raw retained snapshots or general file upload. Trials expire within 24 hours, earlier when the separate 20 MiB tenant/environment staging cap evicts the oldest completed trial. A new run that cannot reserve space after eligible eviction fails before capture. Bound saved estimates to a separate evidence quota (proposed 50 MiB per tenant/environment including lineage), initially private to the author. Attach a default 90-day retention class and apply the governed expiry, hold, purge and tombstone rules. Release retirement prevents fresh runs and leaves retained evidence subject to current access. Replacing a version would erase the forecast someone relied on. Link access alone could expose facts after a role change. [ADR-0078](ADR-0078-governed-documents-files-templates-and-signatures.md)

Store the exact decision and idempotency receipt against actor, scope, release fence and digest. A shared release’s company B run cannot access company A’s data. Writes, including cancel, archive and expiry, use scoped functions with `search_path = pg_catalog, pg_temp` and no direct runtime table grants. Use isolation guards wherever the writer relies on READ COMMITTED. Use one lock order for the tenant/environment quota, person run slot, run and version, including recovery and sweep. System job and expiry keys occupy a separate keyspace from user requests. Atomically couple each committed artifact and audit receipt. This makes a retried submit return the same outcome under fresh authority. A broad background role could cross the company boundary. Writing audit separately could leave a published artifact without its receipt.

## 5. Backtest against what people could have known

Publish an add-only `addForecastBacktestRevision` with stable ID, revision, predecessor, active/retired variants, standard forecast ID and compatible method/configuration contract, historical input and actual query IDs and contracts, and scoring version. Use the **Backtest forecast** Task. Before reading evaluation outcomes, persist the method and assumption revision/digest, scope, horizon, origin-selection rule and exact selected origins, training window rule, evaluation cut and actual contracts. Select the first up to eight consecutive weekly origins in the declared evaluation window whose planned outcome windows are complete at the cut. Select by calendar and horizon without using score or data availability, and keep omissions in the selection. A **Holdout** label requires a trusted immutable record of this plan and its fixed method and assumptions at or before the first held-out origin, plus a tuning window that excludes held-out outcomes. Saved predictions must also predate their outcomes when used in this plan. Show **Retrospective scenario** if the ordering cannot be established or current assumptions are applied to past facts. Per-origin learning under the frozen training rule remains part of the method.

Re-run the same standard calculation on facts effective and recorded by each origin, including purchase promises, bookings and residuals known then. Recompute any learned offsets and cohort eligibility per origin using only its recorded training window. A late-recorded settlement or correction can affect outcomes at the evaluation cut and stays out of historical inputs. A saved-prediction comparison reads only an immutable, authorized **standard** prediction with matching scope, method and output contract. The standard scorer’s other input is a separately governed observed-actuals query of the expected purpose and contract. It rejects Analyze tables as prediction inputs and forecast evidence or Q2 forecast queries as actuals, including transitive bindings. Score comparable completed pairs. Show “Backtest unavailable” and the omitted count for missing authorized historical coverage. A required access denial withholds the entire result using §3. Percentage error would be undefined at zero cash. One score would hide lead-time differences. Scoring against another estimate would leave accuracy untested.

## Owner rulings adopted 2026-11-21

1. **Cash.** Withhold the projection unless every required source reconstructs and reconciles the same opening and accounts for cash movements exactly once. Different refresh times are acceptable with proof of that opening. Matching timestamps alone are insufficient.
2. **Assumptions.** What-ifs are private. A new company default requires reviewed publication. Company and field protection applies to payroll assumptions everywhere the definition or its values are served, including Customize.
3. **Rental.** Start with committed utilisation of the company’s own serialized fleet and show overdue open possession as a capacity risk.
4. **Custom backtests.** Display author-computed scores inside Analyze as **Model-reported**. Standard scoring may make an as-of claim only when the platform independently proves what was known at the origin. The holdout label additionally requires §5’s plan and training evidence.
