## 9. Data/release compatibility and rollback result

Release artifacts and revision/release identities are immutable. Approval
binds an exact pointer base, target content root, factual diff, transition,
compiler/renderer/capability evidence, and current authority epoch. G1 permits
activation or rollback only when the versioned policy admits the exact-pair
no-storage-transition evidence. Rollback is activation of a prior immutable
release through a new human approval and one atomic CAS; it neither reverses
business transactions nor rewrites history. The current rollback/race,
history, request-pinning, and artifact-integrity tests all pass.
