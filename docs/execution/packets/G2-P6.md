## Goal and scope

Test the ERP-factory claim with the first module authored after the press was
built. Catalog is one declarative item entity with:

- tenant/environment case-insensitive SKU identity;
- advisory name resolution;
- required name and base-unit descriptors plus an optional description;
- Q0 get/list/search/resolve, O0 create/update/archive/restore, list/detail/form
  surfaces, agent/reporting/verification projections, and dedicated storage;
- archive/restore as the only removal lifecycle; and
- no on-hand, quantity, stock, valuation, or movement field.

The comparison is intentionally Party-sized rather than a richer Catalog
design. G2-P4 surface conformance, G2-P5 paging/cursors/saved filters, import,
inventory, Location, and every press change remain out of scope.
